The Structural Design Record of BitPangea

The Architecture is the institutional record of the enduring systems through which BitPangea is made viable. It preserves the structural design that connects permanent spatial truth to the systems that define place, Parcels, authority, continuity, compatibility, operation, and experience.

Architectural Purpose The Architecture records what the World requires to remain durable, extensible, coherent, and independently understandable without confusing implementation with constitutional truth.

Architecture Within The Atlas

The Atlas is the canonical map and structural design record of the World. Within it, The Architecture preserves the deep systems that make BitPangea structurally possible without reducing the World to any one implementation, service, interface, institution, or technology.

The architectural domains do not replace one another. Each defines a distinct responsibility and boundary. Together they provide a framework through which permanent place can remain stable while higher systems remain capable of development, replacement, extension, and independent implementation.

Atlas Working Principle The deeper the layer, the more permanent it should be. The closer the layer is to the user, the more freedom it should have to evolve.

Architectural Boundaries

The Architecture is intentionally layered. A lower domain should provide the stable reference or capability needed by higher domains without absorbing the identities, meanings, rights, state, or experiences those higher domains create.

Permanent place should remain independent of ownership. Canonical spatial truth should remain independent of runtime. Runtime should remain independent of any one implementation. Experience should remain free to evolve without redefining the World beneath it.

Atlas Working Principle Extend upward. Do not rewrite downward.
Architectural Direction Ground establishes place. Runtime enables function. Civilization creates meaning.
