**Maintenance Record for BitPangea’s Stewardship Doctrine**

## Purpose

This directory preserves **The Principles**, the foundational stewardship doctrine of BitPangea.

The Principles do not state what is unconditionally true.

They guide how the World should be approached, preserved, interpreted, built upon, and carried forward.

The public `index.html` serves as the active institutional page for this collection.

## Institutional Role

The Principles is one of the eight foundational volumes preserved within **The Foundation**.

Its role is distinct from both **The Truths** and **The Maxims**.

```text
The Truths
→ describe what is

The Principles
→ guide what should be

The Maxims
→ preserve what should be remembered
```

The Principles belong to The Foundation and preserve stewardship doctrine for the World as a whole.

They are not the same as the **Codex Principles**, which preserve lessons revealed through formal Codex inquiry.

## Maintenance Rule

Whenever The Principles is revised:

1. Preserve the exact wording of every adopted Principle unless a deliberate revision is being made.
2. Confirm that numbering remains continuous.
3. Confirm that each Principle remains under the correct thematic section.
4. Preserve the distinction among Truths, Principles, Maxims, and Codex Principles.
5. Verify that explanatory text remains prescriptive rather than constitutional or definitional.
6. Verify the seal path: `/theprinciples/theprinciples.png`.
7. Verify canonical, Open Graph, Twitter, and structured-data metadata.
8. Verify the shared framework stylesheet and script paths.
9. Verify that the shared header and footer load correctly.
10. Confirm that the mobile layout remains readable.
11. Update this README after any addition, removal, renumbering, or thematic reclassification.
12. Record the latest Builder review date below.

## Boundary with Other Institutions

The Principles should not:

- replace The Truths;
- replace The Maxims;
- duplicate the Codex Principles;
- define constitutional terms;
- adopt Codices;
- preserve chronology;
- function as civilizational law;
- become a general list of advice detached from BitPangea’s stewardship architecture.

Its proper role is:

> **The Principles guides how BitPangea should be approached before future civilizational governance exists.**

---

## Addition Standard

Before adding a new Principle, confirm that it is:

- prescriptive rather than descriptive;
- distinct from an existing Principle;
- broader than a domain-specific Codex lesson;
- consistent with stewardship rather than ownership;
- durable enough to remain relevant beyond the moment of adoption;
- appropriate to one of the established thematic sections, or strong enough to justify a new section.

When a new Principle is adopted:

1. assign the next number;
2. place it in the correct thematic section;
3. add a concise interpretive explanation;
4. update this README;
5. record the adoption date in the relevant journal or Chronicle entry when appropriate.
