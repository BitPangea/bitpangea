# The Principles

**BitPangea**  
*A Digital Supercontinent*

---

## Overview

The Principles preserves the stewardship doctrine of BitPangea.

It is one of the foundational works preserved within **The Foundation**.

The Principles do not state what is unconditionally true. They guide how the World should be approached, preserved, interpreted, explored, built upon, and carried forward.

> Truths describe what is. Principles guide what should be. Maxims preserve what should be remembered.

---

## Purpose

The Principles exists to preserve the conduct, restraint, care, and stewardship expected of those who encounter BitPangea.

Its role is prescriptive rather than descriptive.

The Principles asks how a finite World should be handled without reducing stewardship to ownership, discovery to possession, or construction to speed.

---

## Institutional Role

The Principles serves a distinct role within The Foundation:

```text
The Truths
→ constitutional realities

The Principles
→ stewardship doctrine

The Maxims
→ enduring sayings and distilled wisdom
```

The Principles is also distinct from the **Codex Principles**.

The Codex Principles preserve lessons revealed through formal Codex inquiry. The Principles preserves broader stewardship guidance for the World as a whole.

## Repository Guidance

When maintaining this directory:

1. preserve the exact wording of adopted Principles unless a formal revision is made;
2. keep numbering continuous;
3. keep each Principle within its proper thematic section;
4. preserve the distinction among Truths, Principles, Maxims, and Codex Principles;
5. verify all public links, image paths, metadata, and shared framework assets;
6. preserve earlier significant versions before major revisions;
7. keep public documentation free of private maintenance notes or concealed discovery details;
8. document major additions or revisions in the appropriate BitPangea journal or Chronicle entry.

---

## Addition Standard

Before adding a new Principle, confirm that it is:

- prescriptive rather than descriptive;
- distinct from an existing Principle;
- broader than a domain-specific Codex lesson;
- consistent with stewardship rather than ownership;
- durable enough to remain relevant over time;
- appropriate to an existing thematic section or strong enough to justify a new one.

---

## What The Principles Is Not

The Principles is not:

- a substitute for The Truths;
- a substitute for The Maxims;
- a duplicate of the Codex Principles;
- a technical rulebook;
- a chronology;
- a system of civilizational law;
- a general list of advice detached from BitPangea.

Its proper role is:

> **The Principles guides how BitPangea should be approached before future civilizational governance exists.**
