# The Signal

**BitPangea**

## Overview

The Signal preserves brief records of presence from the development of BitPangea.

It exists so future readers may know that someone was here—thinking, building, questioning, and leaving marks before the World was complete.

The Signal is not a news feed or announcement channel.

It is a record of presence.

---

## Purpose

The Signal exists to preserve concise statements that mark a particular moment in the history of BitPangea.

A Signal may record:

- presence;
- uncertainty;
- beginnings;
- unfinished work;
- historical perspective;
- a brief message intended for future readers.

> Long after intentions fade, explanations disappear, and institutions change, signals remain.

---

## Signal 001

**June 2026**

> We were here.  
> The world had not yet been claimed.  
> The map existed only in thought.  
> The future was unknown.

Signal 001 preserves the presence of BitPangea’s early builders during the Foundation Period.

---

## Institutional Role

The Signal is distinct from other BitPangea institutions:

- **The Chronicle** preserves major historical developments.
- **The Journal** preserves detailed day-by-day work.
- **The Archive** preserves records and historical materials.
- **The Transmissions** preserve philosophical and constitutional journeys.
- **The Signal** preserves brief evidence of presence.

The Signal should remain rare and deliberate.

Its importance comes from what a Signal marks, not from how frequently Signals are published.

---

## Future Signals

Additional Signals may appear over time.

No fixed publication schedule exists.

A future Signal should:

1. preserve a meaningful moment of presence;
2. remain concise;
3. include a date or historical period;
4. avoid functioning as routine news or promotion;
5. remain understandable to future readers;
6. be numbered sequentially.

Future records should follow this format:

```text
Signal 001
Signal 002
Signal 003
...
```

## Repository Guidance

When maintaining this directory:

1. preserve the exact wording of adopted Signals unless a formal revision is made;
2. keep Signal numbering sequential;
3. retain the original date or period associated with each Signal;
4. preserve earlier significant versions before major revisions;
5. verify all public links, metadata, and shared framework assets;
6. keep public documentation free of private maintenance notes or concealed discovery details;
7. document major additions or revisions in the appropriate BitPangea journal or Chronicle entry.

---

## What The Signal Is Not

The Signal is not:

- a news feed;
- a changelog;
- a marketing channel;
- a public bulletin;
- a roadmap;
- a substitute for The Chronicle;
- a collection of routine updates.

Its proper role is:

> **The Signal preserves brief evidence that someone was here.**
