## Section Role

Section XIV describes how BitPangea should advance from present architectural work toward a World capable of supporting Builders, participation, civilization, and history.

It does not present a calendar roadmap.

It presents a dependency path.

The central rule is:

> **Architecture should determine readiness. A schedule should not manufacture it.**

The next step should therefore be determined by mature architectural dependency rather than by an old sequence or artificial deadline.

---

## Progress by Dependency, Not by Calendar

The section rejects schedule-driven development.

Some work may proceed in parallel.

Other work must wait for deeper architectural prerequisites.

The section preserves:

> **The next step is the work whose dependencies are mature enough to support it.**

This means BitPangea advances through demonstrated necessity rather than arbitrary sequence.

---

## Resolve Spatial Truth

The deepest remaining work begins with spatial Architecture.

The section preserves the demonstrated dependency:

```text
Foundational Survey Fabric
↓
Spatial Ground
↓
General Spatial Interpretation
↓
Parcel Cadastre
```

### Foundational Survey Fabric

Establishes canonical spatial reference.

Its final mathematics and implementation remain unresolved in White Paper Version 0.1.

### Spatial Ground

Must determine how canonical Survey reference becomes usable World-space ground.

### General Spatial Interpretation

Must establish higher spatial meaning without redefining deeper canonical truth.

### Parcel Cadastre

Must preserve canonical Parcel identity and territory.

The section identifies this spatial sequence as strict because its dependencies have already been demonstrated.

---

## Spatial Truth Before Parcel Proof

The immediate objective is not to complete every future spatial detail.

It is to establish enough exact spatial truth that the Parcel architecture can stand without ambiguity.

The path is therefore:

```text
canonical spatial reference
↓
World-space ground
↓
higher spatial meaning
↓
canonical Parcel truth
```

---

## Prove the Parcel Architecture

The Parcel architecture must eventually move from design direction to mathematical proof.

BitPangea must demonstrate that exactly **21,000,000 Parcels** can exist while preserving established canonical Parcel properties.

The final geometry must support:

- persistent Parcel identity;
- fixed canonical position;
- non-overlap;
- indivisibility;
- shared Boundary where Parcel Adjacency exists;
- participation of all Parcels in one World-scale Parcel Contiguity.

The section preserves the present rhombic grammar as a leading visible design direction.

It is not yet the proof.

---

## Geometry Must Conform to the Architecture

The section preserves the principle:

> **The geometry must conform to the architecture.**

The fixed Parcel count and established Parcel properties constrain the geometry.

A preferred geometry does not redefine those deeper requirements.

Only after compatibility is demonstrated should a Parcel design be treated as mature enough for implementation.

---

## Establish the Cadastre

Once spatial truth and Parcel geometry are sufficiently established, the Parcel Cadastre can become the authoritative source of canonical Parcel identity and territory.

Its governing question is:

> **Which Parcel is this, where is it fixed, and what territory canonically belongs to it?**

The Cadastre is presented as a major threshold between spatial Architecture and later operational systems.

It does not decide:

- ownership;
- economic value;
- permitted activity.

Its responsibility is:

> **to preserve Parcel truth**

---

## Establish Operational Authority and Change

Beyond the demonstrated spatial chain, development becomes less linear.

The section names:

- Identity / Rights / Control;
- Persistence / Provenance;
- Interoperability;
- World Runtime

as recognized Architecture domains whose relationships must be demonstrated rather than assumed.

The White Paper therefore rejects forcing these domains into one permanent simplistic sequence.

Their development should follow actual dependency as that dependency becomes known.

---

## Only Claim Strict Ordering Where Demonstrated

The section preserves:

> **Only claim strict ordering where dependency has actually been demonstrated.**

This is especially important beyond the spatial chain.

Identity / Rights / Control must determine:

- who or what is acting;
- what recognized relationship exists;
- what authority may be exercised.

World Runtime must process legitimate change without becoming the source of authority.

Persistence / Provenance must preserve continuity and evidence.

Interoperability must preserve one canonical meaning across conforming implementations.

Their exact internal dependency relationships remain matters for architectural inquiry.

---

## Enable Builders

Meaningful Builder participation should expand only when the World can reliably preserve:

- place;
- authority;
- legitimate change;
- continuity.

The threshold is not simply whether software allows creation.

The question is whether Builder activity can occur without making the World incoherent.

The governing Builder principle is:

> **Create within the World without redefining the World.**

Builders may eventually create:

- structures;
- services;
- institutions;
- infrastructure;
- experiences;
- persistent activity.

They should do so within established World Architecture.

---

## Widen Participation

Participation should expand as Architecture becomes capable of supporting it coherently.

Experience Architecture may eventually support different ways of:

- navigating;
- observing;
- building;
- communicating;
- interacting.

The section preserves the idea that user-facing systems should remain more evolvable than the deeper Architecture beneath them.

The participant should encounter a World rather than a stack diagram.

Technical complexity should remain below the experience wherever possible.

---

## Creator to Steward

As participation expands, the Creator’s role should increasingly move from direct authorship toward stewardship.

BitPangea should become progressively less dependent upon one person for what happens within it.

The deeper World remains intentionally designed.

Its future activity should increasingly emerge from participants operating within that design.

---

## Allow Civilization and History to Emerge

Civilization is not the final feature on a roadmap.

It may emerge after BitPangea becomes capable of sustaining persistent activity.

The section identifies possible outcomes such as:

- communities;
- institutions;
- economies;
- meaningful places;
- traditions;
- conflict;
- cooperation;
- memory;
- history.

These should not receive artificial completion dates.

BitPangea can create the conditions for civilization.

It cannot schedule authentic civilization into existence.

---

## History Requires Endurance

The same principle applies to history.

History begins when consequences accumulate and later participants inherit evidence of what occurred before them.

The World becomes old by enduring.

History therefore cannot be scheduled as a development milestone.

It must accumulate through persistent continuity.

---

## The Dependency Path

The section summarizes the long-term conceptual path as:

```text
Resolve spatial truth
↓
prove the Parcel architecture
↓
establish Cadastre
↓
establish legitimate authority and change
↓
enable Builders
↓
widen participation
↓
allow civilization and history to emerge
```

This is explicitly:

> **a dependency path**

not:

> a rigid implementation schedule.

Different Architecture domains may advance in parallel once their prerequisites are sufficiently understood.

Some questions may return.

Others may remain intentionally open.

---

## The Days Record Progress

The section preserves an important Creator Period discipline:

> **The Days record progress. They do not command progress.**

Creator Period chronology records what happened.

It should not become a scheduling mechanism that forces the Architecture forward prematurely.

---

The section closes by pairing two principles:

and:

Together they define the development discipline of Section XIV.
