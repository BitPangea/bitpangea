## Section Role

Section XIII gives a status-oriented snapshot of BitPangea as presented in White Paper Version 0.1.

It explains that BitPangea is neither:

```text
a blank concept
```

nor:

```text
a finished system
```

Instead, the project contains different kinds of certainty and maturity.

The purpose of the section is to make clear:

- what has actually been established;
- what is recognized Architecture still being designed;
- what is provisional or parked;
- what is deliberately left unresolved for future civilization.

---

## The Four-Part Status Framework

The section preserves four distinct states:

### Established

Truths, principles, and strong directions already sufficiently settled to constrain future work.

### Recognized Architecture — In Development

Enduring responsibilities already known to belong to BitPangea’s Architecture, even where internal design remains incomplete.

### Provisional / Parked

Promising, leading, deferred, or dependency-bound design work that has not yet earned equivalent architectural standing.

### Intentionally Open

Questions and outcomes deliberately left for future Builders, institutions, communities, and civilization rather than predetermined by the Creator.

The distinction between the middle two categories is especially important.

A recognized Architecture domain is an enduring responsibility.

A provisional or parked concept may still be important, but it has not yet earned the same standing.

---

## Established

Section XIII identifies several truths and principles as established enough to constrain later work.

These include:

- BitPangea is one finite, complete, continuous cartographic World;
- BitPangea contains exactly **21,000,000 Parcels**;
- each canonical Parcel has persistent identity and fixed position;
- Parcels do not overlap;
- canonical Parcels are indivisible;
- Boundaries distinguish Parcels;
- Parcel Adjacency is established through shared Boundary;
- all Parcels participate in one World-scale Parcel Contiguity;
- World finitude does not depend on ownership, occupation, development, or representation;
- existence of place precedes later economic or social relationships.

---

## Demonstrated Spatial Dependency

The section preserves the strongest demonstrated strict dependency sequence as:

```text
Foundational Survey Fabric
↓
Spatial Ground
↓
General Spatial Interpretation
↓
Parcel Cadastre
```

It identifies this as the clearest demonstrated strict dependency within The Architecture.

This sequence should not be generalized into a universal linear ordering for all Architecture domains.

---

## Established Principles

The section explicitly preserves:

> **Representation may change. Place must not.**

> **Reference does not transfer authority.**

> **Create within the World without redefining the World.**

> **Architecture enables. Civilization emerges.**

> **The deeper the layer, the more permanent it should be. The closer the layer is to the user, the more freedom it should have to evolve.**

These principles already constrain later design even where specific implementation work remains incomplete.

---

## Recognized Architecture — In Development

The section identifies the following responsibilities as recognized Architecture still under development:

- Spatial Ground;
- General Spatial Interpretation;
- Parcel Cadastre;
- Identity / Rights / Control;
- Persistence / Provenance;
- Interoperability;
- World Runtime;
- Experience Architecture.

Recognition means:

> **The responsibility is real. The final design is not yet complete.**

This is stronger than speculation.

It is weaker than completion.

---

## Foundational Survey Fabric Status

The section describes the **Foundational Survey Fabric** as further advanced in institutional definition than the other developing Architecture domains.

Its final mathematics and implementation remain deliberately unresolved in White Paper Version 0.1.

That distinction should remain visible:

```text
institutional responsibility
→ established more strongly

final mathematics / implementation
→ unresolved
```

---

## World Runtime Status

World Runtime is recognized as necessary.

Its internal architecture remains reserved for later development.

The enduring responsibility is known.

The implementation and detailed internal model are not presented as complete.

---

## Identity / Rights / Control Status

Identity / Rights / Control has established conceptual boundaries.

The section does not claim a final:

- rights taxonomy;
- ownership model.

Its recognized responsibility is stronger than its current internal design maturity.

---

## Persistence / Provenance Status

Persistence / Provenance is recognized as essential for:

- continuity;
- historical accountability;
- lineage;
- evidence.

Its complete operational form remains under development.

---

## Interoperability Status

Interoperability is recognized as necessary if multiple conforming implementations are to preserve one canonical meaning.

The mechanisms through which that conformance will be achieved remain under development.

---

## Experience Architecture Status

Experience Architecture is recognized because participants will eventually require ways to:

- perceive;
- navigate;
- interact with;
- inhabit

the World.

The section also preserves that interfaces should remain among the most evolvable parts of BitPangea.

---

## Provisional / Parked

The section identifies several areas as provisional, exploratory, or parked.

### Parcel Geometry

The leading visible Parcel direction is:

> a three-orientation rhombic grammar derived from a three-part hexagonal construction

It remains important.

It is not yet final mathematical specification.

### World Form

BitPangea is presently designed as a:

> **singular, compositionally balanced, asymmetric digital supercontinent**

That direction is strong enough to guide design.

But terminal morphology remains under refinement.

### The Verge

The exact expression of **The Verge** remains design space rather than constitutional truth.

### Outliers and Non-Parcel Transitions

Possible Outliers and non-Parcel transitions remain open design territory.

---

## Parked Questions

The section identifies earlier inquiries such as:

- Density;
- Pattern;
- Distribution;
- Void;
- Compactness;

as examples of questions that may remain parked until dependency makes them necessary again.

Parking is not abandonment.

It is a decision not to force resolution before the Architecture is ready.

The governing rule is:

> **Dependency before schedule.**

A parked question should return because the Architecture requires it, not because an old roadmap says it is next.

---

## Intentionally Open

Some questions should remain open even after Architecture matures.

The section treats this as design restraint rather than incompleteness.

BitPangea should not predetermine:

- which communities will form;
- which cultures will emerge;
- which institutions participants will create;
- which Parcels will become important;
- what economic relationships will dominate;
- which traditions will survive;
- how history will later be interpreted;
- what future civilization will value.

These are spaces preserved for civilization.

---

## Finite Supply Does Not Dictate Economic Outcome

Exactly **21,000,000 Parcels** establish finite spatial supply.

That does not determine:

- future price;
- demand;
- ownership distribution;
- rent;
- trade;
- economic hierarchy;
- social significance.

The World may create conditions under which those outcomes emerge.

It should not author those outcomes in advance.

---

## Historical Interpretation Remains Open

BitPangea may preserve evidence without requiring one permanent interpretation of that evidence.

Likewise:

- institutions may emerge without the Architecture deciding which institutions deserve cultural authority;
- Builders may be enabled without the Architecture deciding what they must create.

These are intentional openings, not architectural gaps.

---

## Unresolved Does Not Mean Undefined

The section preserves one of BitPangea’s most important status principles:

> **Unresolved does not mean undefined.**

An unresolved question may already be heavily constrained by deeper truth.

Examples include:

### Parcel Geometry

Not finalized, but must still support:

- exactly **21,000,000 Parcels**;
- persistent canonical identity;
- non-overlap;
- Parcel Adjacency through shared Boundary;
- World-scale Parcel Contiguity.

### World Runtime

Not fully designed, but must:

> **permit legitimate change without surrendering continuity**

### Identity / Rights / Control

Not finalized, but cannot collapse:

- identity;
- rights;
- ownership;
- permission;
- control;
- authority

into synonyms.

### Experience Architecture

Remains open, but cannot redefine canonical spatial truth merely because an interface represents something differently.

---

## Disciplined Open Architecture

Section XIII presents BitPangea as a combination of permanence and restraint.

Some truths are established strongly enough to govern everything above them.

Some Architecture domains are recognized but still being developed.

Some design directions remain provisional or parked.

Some outcomes should never be predetermined.

The objective is not to resolve every question as quickly as possible.

It is to resolve each question:

- at the correct level of authority;
- at the correct time;
- with the correct degree of permanence.

---

## Development Discipline

The section closes with:

> **Establish what must endure.**

> **Develop what must operate.**

> **Test what remains provisional.**

> **Leave open what should emerge.**

This is the governing discipline for an open but bounded BitPangea Architecture.
