# The Frontier Office

**BitPangea**  
*A Digital Supercontinent*

---

## Overview

The Frontier Office is the primary public communications point of BitPangea.

It exists as the place where questions, ideas, criticism, research, and observations may be received during the World’s development.

The Frontier Office preserves communication without turning correspondence into constitutional authority.

---

## Purpose

The Frontier Office exists to facilitate communication between BitPangea and those exploring it.

Its role includes receiving:

- questions about the World;
- constructive criticism;
- research inquiries;
- long-term worldbuilding ideas;
- governance and institutional questions;
- observations from the edge of the project;
- correspondence that may help clarify future inquiry.

> The future is not built in a day.  
> It begins with a question.

---

## Institutional Role

The Frontier Office is distinct from other BitPangea institutions:

- **The Charter** preserves constitutional purpose and direction.
- **The Codex** preserves formal inquiry and recognized knowledge.
- **The Chronicle** preserves history.
- **The Commons** preserves public discussion.
- **The Frontier Office** receives and preserves communication.

The Frontier Office may receive ideas that later influence discussion or inquiry, but correspondence does not become adopted merely because it is received.

---

## What Belongs Here

The Frontier Office may receive:

- questions about BitPangea;
- thoughtful criticism;
- relevant research;
- long-term civilization and worldbuilding ideas;
- questions about governance, stewardship, permanence, and geography;
- observations concerning the public architecture of the project;
- correspondence intended for future consideration.

---

## What Does Not Belong

The Frontier Office is not intended for:

- token requests;
- investment solicitations;
- Parcel reservations;
- land-sale inquiries;
- speculative pricing questions;
- spam;
- unrelated promotion;
- claims that correspondence creates rights, allocations, or constitutional standing.

Its purpose is communication, not commerce or allocation.

---

## Communication Standard

Material received through The Frontier Office should be:

1. relevant to BitPangea;
2. clear enough to understand without private context;
3. respectful and constructive;
4. identified as inquiry, criticism, research, or observation;
5. free of unsupported claims of authority or entitlement;
6. suitable for preservation when historically or institutionally meaningful.

Not every message requires publication or permanent preservation.

---

## Public Structure

This directory may include:

- the primary Frontier Office page;
- public contact or inquiry guidance;
- supporting communication records intended for public viewing;
- this public repository README.

Private correspondence, personal information, internal review notes, and concealed discovery details should not be placed in the public repository.

---

## Repository Guidance

When maintaining this directory:

1. keep public contact information current;
2. use clear and stable filenames;
3. avoid publishing private correspondence without permission;
4. remove personal information from any public record unless publication is intentional and appropriate;
5. preserve earlier significant versions when the institution materially changes;
6. verify all public links, forms, assets, and metadata;
7. keep public documentation free of private maintenance notes or concealed discovery details;
8. document major institutional changes in the appropriate BitPangea journal or Chronicle entry.

---

## What The Frontier Office Is Not

The Frontier Office is not:

- a governing authority;
- a public forum;
- a voting system;
- a marketplace;
- a Parcel reservation office;
- a support desk for a finalized product;
- a substitute for The Commons;
- a guarantee that submitted ideas will be adopted or answered.

Its proper role is:

> **To receive communication from the edge of the World and preserve what may matter.**
