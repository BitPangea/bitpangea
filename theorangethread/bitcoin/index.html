Before BitPangea could imagine finite digital land, verifiable ownership, cryptographic permanence, or a world without central authority, Bitcoin demonstrated that such things could exist.

This tribute does not honor price, speculation, or possession. It honors the first enduring proof that a digital system could establish scarcity, truth, and transfer without asking permission from a sovereign center.

Bitcoin did not create BitPangea. It made a world like BitPangea conceivable.
