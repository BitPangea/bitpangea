# The Orange Thread

**A concealed lineage of Bitcoin-related tributes within BitPangea**

The Orange Thread preserves selected acknowledgments of Bitcoin and of Bitcoin-related technologies, principles, or structures that meaningfully enter the architecture of BitPangea.

It is not a general history of Bitcoin, a catalog of terminology, or a collection organized around market value, price, speculation, or possession. Its purpose is narrower: to recognize those ideas and technologies that become part of BitPangea’s lived constitutional, technical, or experiential development.

---

## Purpose

Bitcoin begins The Orange Thread because its influence precedes BitPangea itself.

The first tribute recognizes Bitcoin as an enduring demonstration that digital scarcity, cryptographic verification, verifiable transfer, and operation without a sovereign center could exist. In that sense, Bitcoin did not create BitPangea, but it helped make a world like BitPangea conceivable.

Any future tribute may be created only when a Bitcoin-related technology, principle, or structure has been meaningfully implemented within BitPangea.

Implementation establishes eligibility. It does not require a tribute.

The World must also reveal a natural and meaningful place for that tribute to exist.

---

## Governing Principles

The Orange Thread is governed by restraint.

- Bitcoin is the first and presently only tribute.
- No exhaustive list of future tributes is maintained.
- No tribute is created merely because a concept is important to Bitcoin.
- A related technology or principle must first become meaningfully implemented within BitPangea.
- The existence of a tribute does not require public explanation of how it is discovered.
- The Thread may remain unchanged indefinitely if no further qualifying implementation occurs.

---

## First Tribute

### Bitcoin

The first tribute within The Orange Thread is dedicated to Bitcoin.

It honors Bitcoin not as an investment, market, or speculative asset, but as a foundational digital idea whose architecture demonstrated:

- durable digital scarcity;
- cryptographic verification;
- permissionless transfer;
- decentralized operation;
- persistence without dependence on a sovereign center.

The tribute is preserved as part of BitPangea’s public world while its discovery architecture remains outside the scope of this public repository record.
