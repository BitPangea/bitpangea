# BitPangea Transmissions

**BitPangea**  
*A Digital Supercontinent*

---

## Overview

The Transmissions preserve a sequence of philosophical, constitutional, and experiential works through which BitPangea’s developing ideas are presented over time.

Each Transmission has its own title, theme, format, and relationship to the broader development of the World.

The series is cumulative, but individual Transmissions are not required to share the same visual or narrative structure.

---

## Purpose

The Transmissions exist to preserve the unfolding thought of BitPangea.

Some Transmissions function as statements.

Some function as thresholds.

Some function as journeys.

Each one should use the form best suited to the idea it carries.

## Directory Standard

Transmission directories should follow this naming pattern:

```text
transmission###-title
```

Examples:

```text
transmission001-whatif
transmission002-themap
transmission003-thefrontier
transmission004-thebuilder
transmission005-theinheritance
transmission006-thebetween
transmission007-thepresence
```

Use:

- a three-digit Transmission number;
- lowercase letters;
- hyphens between words;
- stable numbering after publication.

---

## Media Principles

Transmissions are not required to use a single format.

Possible forms include:

- static narrative pages;
- interactive path-based experiences;
- meditative scroll journeys;
- cinematic or documentary presentations;
- minimalist visual sequences;
- animation embedded directly within HTML.

The medium should serve the constitutional or philosophical purpose of the individual Transmission.

Form should never overwhelm meaning.

---

## Public Structure

This directory may include:

- numbered Transmission folders;
- a public landing or index page;
- shared Transmission assets;
- this public repository README.

Each Transmission should remain self-contained enough to preserve its identity while still belonging clearly to the larger series.

---

## Repository Guidance

When maintaining this directory:

1. preserve Transmission numbering and titles;
2. keep directory names aligned with public titles;
3. verify page titles, canonical URLs, metadata, and asset paths;
4. preserve earlier significant versions before major revisions;
5. keep public documentation free of private maintenance notes or concealed discovery details;
6. avoid renaming or relocating published Transmissions without preserving continuity;
7. document major additions or revisions in the appropriate BitPangea journal or Chronicle entry;
8. maintain the series as a coherent progression rather than a collection of unrelated pages.

---

## What The Transmissions Are Not

The Transmissions are not:

- a conventional news series;
- a changelog;
- a technical specification;
- a roadmap;
- a marketing campaign;
- a substitute for The Chronicle or The Codex.

Their purpose is to preserve thought, transition, and experience.
