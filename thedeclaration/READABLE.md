## Institutional Role

The Declaration precedes The Charter and The Codex.

Its function is to declare foundational truth before that truth is later organized, interpreted, or preserved through other institutions.


The Declaration should therefore remain concise, foundational, and resistant to unnecessary expansion.

## Boundary with Other Institutions

The Declaration should not:

- function as The Charter;
- contain extended constitutional history;
- adopt Codices;
- define the complete vocabulary of BitPangea;
- preserve chronology;
- replace The Foundation;
- become a general manifesto for every future idea.

Its role is narrower:

> **The Declaration states what must be true before the rest of BitPangea can proceed.**
