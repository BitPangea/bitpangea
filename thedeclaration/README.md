# The Declaration

**BitPangea**  
*A Digital Supercontinent*

---

## Overview

The Declaration preserves the foundational truths upon which BitPangea is established.

It precedes formal Codex inquiry and states the constitutional premises that define the World before later cartographic, relational, institutional, and design structures are developed.

Beginning with the Creator Period, these premises are understood as the enduring constitutional reality within which intentional world design may proceed.

The Declaration is concise by design.

---

## Foundational Declaration

BitPangea is:

- one World;
- composed of 21,000,000 Parcels;
- finite;
- incapable of expansion;
- incapable of duplication;
- a single digital supercontinent.

These statements are not presented as later discoveries.

They are the foundational truths from which the rest of BitPangea proceeds.

> The World exists. The map follows.

Under the Creator Period clarification, this doctrine means that the World’s constitutional identity and invariants come first. The Atlas may then intentionally design the concrete cartographic expression of that World within those constraints.

---

## Purpose

The Declaration exists to preserve the constitutional beginning of BitPangea.

Its role is to establish what must already be true before:

- the World can be mapped;
- Parcels can be described;
- relations can be recognized;
- Codices can be adopted;
- intentional world design can proceed;
- institutions can develop;
- history can be preserved.

The Declaration should remain stable, direct, and resistant to unnecessary expansion.

It should not be expanded merely to govern design choices that properly belong to The Atlas or implementation.

---

## Institutional Role

The Declaration is distinct from other BitPangea institutions:

- **The Declaration** proclaims the foundational truths of the World.
- **The Charter** preserves purpose and constitutional direction.
- **The Codex** preserves constitutional inquiry and concepts requiring canonical stability.
- **The Lexicon** preserves the language of the World.
- **The Atlas** intentionally designs the concrete cartographic expression of the World within constitutional constraints.
- **The Archive** preserves historically important materials.
- **The Chronicle** preserves the sequence of development.

The Declaration should not duplicate those institutions.

Its purpose is foundational recognition.

The governing relationship is:

**The Declaration establishes → The Foundation preserves → The Codex defines → The Atlas designs → Implementation realizes.**

---

## Creator Period Clarification

The Creator Period does not revise the foundational declaration itself.

The six foundational statements remain unchanged:

- one World;
- 21,000,000 Parcels;
- finite;
- no expansion;
- no duplication;
- a single digital supercontinent.

What changed is the interpretation of how those truths relate to design.

The Declaration establishes what must already be constitutionally true. It does not require every geographic, visual, naming, cartographic, or technical feature of BitPangea to have existed in fully determined form before the Creator begins to design.

The distinction is:

> **Constitutional reality precedes design. Design gives intentional form to what the Constitution leaves open.**

A design decision should return to constitutional inquiry only if it later creates a genuine dependency upon identity, permanence, Structure, Relation, World Properties, or another foundational truth.

## What The Declaration Is Not

The Declaration is not:

- a technical specification;
- a cartographic design manual;
- a roadmap;
- a policy manual;
- a complete explanation of BitPangea;
- a substitute for The Charter;
- a substitute for The Codex;
- a substitute for The Atlas;
- a place for unresolved questions;
- a requirement that every feature of the World be constitutionally predetermined before it may be designed.

Its role is to preserve the foundational truths from which the World becomes intelligible and within which intentional design may proceed.
