# The Declaration

**BitPangea**  
*A Digital Supercontinent*

---

## Overview

The Declaration preserves the foundational truths upon which BitPangea is established.

It precedes formal Codex inquiry and states the constitutional premises that define the World before later cartographic, relational, and institutional structures are recognized.

The Declaration is concise by design.

---

## Foundational Declaration

BitPangea is:

- one World;
- composed of 21,000,000 Parcels;
- finite;
- incapable of expansion;
- incapable of duplication;
- a single digital supercontinent.

These statements are not presented as later discoveries.

They are the foundational truths from which the rest of BitPangea proceeds.

> The World exists. The map follows.

---

## Purpose

The Declaration exists to preserve the constitutional beginning of BitPangea.

Its role is to establish what must already be true before:

- the World can be mapped;
- Parcels can be described;
- relations can be recognized;
- Codices can be adopted;
- institutions can develop;
- history can be preserved.

The Declaration should remain stable, direct, and resistant to unnecessary expansion.

---

## Institutional Role

The Declaration is distinct from other BitPangea institutions:

- **The Declaration** proclaims the foundational truths of the World.
- **The Charter** preserves purpose and constitutional direction.
- **The Codex** preserves structured inquiry and recognized knowledge.
- **The Lexicon** preserves the language of the World.
- **The Archive** preserves historically important materials.
- **The Chronicle** preserves the sequence of development.

The Declaration should not duplicate those institutions.

Its purpose is foundational recognition.

## Repository Guidance

When maintaining this directory:

1. preserve the wording and order of the foundational statements unless a formal constitutional revision is made;
2. keep filenames and public paths stable;
3. preserve earlier significant versions before major revisions;
4. verify all public links, image paths, and metadata;
5. keep the supporting **One World** material consistent with the main Declaration;
6. keep public documentation free of private maintenance notes or concealed discovery details;
7. document major constitutional changes in the appropriate BitPangea journal or Chronicle entry.

---

## What The Declaration Is Not

The Declaration is not:

- a technical specification;
- a roadmap;
- a policy manual;
- a complete explanation of BitPangea;
- a substitute for The Charter;
- a substitute for The Codex;
- a place for unresolved questions.

Its role is to preserve the foundational truths from which the World becomes intelligible.
