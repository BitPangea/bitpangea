## Record Role

This page preserves **Binary** as the first dependency in The Luminaries.

The record does not present binary merely as arithmetic.

Its importance is representational.

Binary demonstrates that complex meaning can be encoded through repeated arrangements of two states.

That representational possibility becomes a prerequisite for later digital systems.

---

## The Discovery

The record attributes the modern binary number system to:

> **Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz**

The page describes Leibniz as having developed and described a binary system based upon:

```text
1
0
```

The significance preserved by the record is not simply that two symbols can express numbers.

It is that complexity can be reduced into combinations of two distinguishable states.

---

## What Became Possible

The page identifies binary as foundational to later digital representation.

It states that combinations of two states eventually made it possible to represent:

- numbers;
- instructions;
- memory;
- images;
- sound;
- language;
- identity;
- verification;
- ownership.

The larger principle is:

> **The modern digital world does not merely use binary. It depends upon the idea that meaning can be encoded through difference.**
