# The Luminaries

**BitPangea**  
*A Digital Supercontinent*

---

## Overview

The Luminaries preserves an ordered lineage of foundational digital ideas.

Each Luminary represents an idea that helped make the Digital World possible. Together, the sequence presents those ideas as an intellectual inheritance rather than as isolated inventions or a general history of technology.

The public `index.html` serves as the primary entry point into the series.

---

## Purpose

The Luminaries exists to preserve the sequence of ideas through which the Digital World became possible.

Each entry should help explain:

- what idea is being preserved;
- why it mattered;
- what later possibility it enabled;
- how it relates to the Luminaries before and after it.

The sequence is intended to remain cumulative.

Each Luminary should feel like an inheritance passed forward.

## Institutional Role

The Luminaries is not a general encyclopedia of technology.

It preserves a specific lineage of ideas that contributed to the emergence of the Digital World.

Its role is distinct from other BitPangea institutions:

- **The Codex** preserves structured inquiry and recognized knowledge.
- **The Archive** preserves historically important materials.
- **The Reliquary** preserves artifacts and discoverable records.
- **The Luminaries** preserves the ordered inheritance of foundational digital ideas.

## What The Luminaries Is Not

The Luminaries is not:

- a list of famous people;
- a general technology timeline;
- a catalog of inventions without lineage;
- a substitute for The Codex;
- a miscellaneous archive of digital history.

Its purpose is narrower:

> **The Luminaries preserves the ordered inheritance of ideas that made the Digital World possible.**
