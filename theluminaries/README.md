## Purpose

This directory preserves **The Luminaries**, BitPangea’s ordered lineage of foundational digital ideas.

The Luminaries presents a sequence through which major concepts of the Digital World are preserved as distinct intellectual inheritances. Each Luminary occupies its own numbered directory and contributes to the broader historical and philosophical record of BitPangea.

The public `index.html` serves as the primary entry point into the series.

## Directory Standard

Luminary directories follow this naming pattern:

```text
##-descriptive-title
```

Examples:

```text
01-binary
02-logic
03-computation
04-information
05-public-key-cryptography
06-digital-cash
07-programmable-agreements
08-digital-scarcity
09-not-yet-revealed
```

Use:

- two-digit numbering;
- lowercase letters;
- hyphens between words;
- no spaces;
- stable numbering once published.

---

## Institutional Role

The Luminaries preserves a lineage of ideas rather than a general encyclopedia of technology.

Each entry should answer, in its own way:

- What idea is being preserved?
- Why did it matter to the emergence of the Digital World?
- What later possibility did it make possible?
- How does it connect to the Luminary before and after it?

The sequence should remain cumulative.

Each Luminary should feel like an inheritance passed forward rather than an isolated topic.

## Boundary

The Luminaries should not become:

- a general technology timeline;
- a list of famous people;
- a catalog of inventions without lineage;
- a substitute for The Codex;
- a substitute for The Reliquary;
- a miscellaneous archive of digital history.

Its purpose is narrower:

> **The Luminaries preserves the ordered inheritance of ideas that made the Digital World possible.**
