# The Commons

**BitPangea**  
*A Digital Supercontinent*

---

## Overview

The Commons is the public gathering place of BitPangea.

It exists to preserve thoughtful questions, criticism, exploration, and discussion while the World remains under development.

The Commons provides space for ideas to be examined before they become institutions, rules, or permanent structures.

---

## Purpose

The Commons exists as a place where inquiry can gather.

It encourages:

- thoughtful questions;
- constructive criticism;
- research;
- exploration;
- discussion;
- historical comparison;
- long-term thinking.

Its role is public and deliberative.

The Commons does not decide constitutional questions. It preserves the discussion through which better questions and clearer understanding may emerge.

---

## What Belongs in The Commons

The Commons may include:

- questions about finite digital geography;
- criticism of BitPangea’s concepts or assumptions;
- ideas concerning stewardship, permanence, and governance;
- long-term civilization and worldbuilding questions;
- historical parallels and relevant research;
- discussion of what should remain fixed and what should remain revisable;
- ideas that may later inform formal inquiry elsewhere in BitPangea.

---

## What Does Not Belong

The Commons is not intended for:

- token requests;
- investment solicitations;
- Parcel reservations;
- land-sale inquiries;
- speculative price discussion;
- spam;
- unrelated promotion;
- hype presented as constitutional thought.

Its purpose is inquiry, not speculation.

---

## Foundational Questions

Questions preserved through The Commons may include:

- Why should digital geography be finite?
- What does it mean for a World to remain permanent?
- What rules should never change?
- Can a digital World possess a constitutional core?
- What responsibilities accompany stewardship?
- What kind of civilization might emerge from finite digital geography?
- What belongs within the World, and what should remain outside it?

These questions are not automatically answered or adopted merely because they appear in The Commons.

---

## Institutional Role

The Commons is distinct from other BitPangea institutions:

- **The Charter** preserves constitutional purpose and direction.
- **The Codex** preserves formal inquiry and recognized knowledge.
- **The Chronicle** preserves history.
- **The Archive** preserves records and historical materials.
- **The Frontier Office** receives communication.
- **The Commons** preserves public discussion.

The Commons should remain a gathering place rather than becoming an authority over the World.

---

## Discussion Standard

Material preserved in The Commons should be:

1. relevant to BitPangea;
2. thoughtful rather than promotional;
3. clear about whether it is a question, criticism, proposal, or reference;
4. open to revision;
5. respectful of the distinction between public discussion and constitutional adoption;
6. understandable without private context.

---

## Repository Guidance

When maintaining this directory:

1. use clear, stable filenames and paths;
2. distinguish active discussion from preserved historical material;
3. avoid presenting unresolved ideas as adopted facts;
4. preserve meaningful earlier versions when major revisions occur;
5. verify all public links and asset paths;
6. keep public documentation free of private maintenance notes or concealed discovery details;
7. document major institutional changes in the appropriate BitPangea journal or Chronicle entry.

---

## What The Commons Is Not

The Commons is not:

- a governing body;
- a voting system;
- a marketplace;
- a roadmap;
- a technical specification;
- a substitute for The Codex;
- a place where discussion automatically becomes constitutional authority.

Its purpose is to preserve the space in which questions can gather.

> Before there are cities, markets, or maps, there must be a place where questions can gather.
