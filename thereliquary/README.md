## Institutional Role

The Reliquary preserves hidden and discoverable records that do not belong to the ordinary visible structure of BitPangea.

Its role is distinct from:

- **The Archive**, which preserves historical and constitutional records;
- **The Chronicle**, which preserves chronology;
- **The Codex**, which preserves canonical knowledge;
- **The Charter**, which preserves constitutional development;
- **The Foundation**, which preserves the constitutional library.

The Reliquary instead preserves artifacts whose discovery is itself part of the experience.

Its governing principle is:

> **The Reliquary does not reveal the path. It confirms that paths exist.**

## Maintenance Rule

Whenever The Reliquary or one of its records is revised:

1. Verify the public threshold page.
2. Verify the concealed `relics` link to The Mainframe.
3. Verify all affected folder and file paths.
4. Confirm that canonical, Open Graph, Twitter, and structured-data metadata remain accurate.
5. Confirm that hidden-link styling does not visually expose concealed triggers unless intentional.
6. Verify all multi-stage discovery routes leading to the affected record.
7. Update **The Reliquary Ledger**.
8. Record the new verification date.
9. Preserve previous paths when a record is renamed or relocated.
10. Add redirects where practical.
11. Confirm that public pages do not disclose the complete hidden architecture.

## Boundary

The Reliquary should not become:

- a conventional public directory;
- a visible sitemap;
- a substitute for The Archive;
- a general media library;
- a repository for unfinished drafts without preservation value;
- a place where concealed paths are publicly documented;
- an institutional authority over the records it preserves.

Its purpose is preservation through discovery.

Folder names should be:

3. update hidden source links;
4. update the Reliquary Ledger;
5. preserve the previous route where practical.
