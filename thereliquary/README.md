# The Reliquary

**BitPangea**  
*A Digital Supercontinent*

---

## Overview

The Reliquary is BitPangea’s preservation layer for artifacts, inscriptions, monuments, glyphs, interpretations, fragments, relics, and other records whose discovery is part of their meaning.

It is not intended to function as a conventional public index.

> The Reliquary does not reveal the path. It confirms that paths exist.

---

## Purpose

The Reliquary exists to preserve works that belong outside the ordinary visible structure of BitPangea.

Its role is to protect records whose value depends not only on what they contain, but also on how they are encountered.

The Reliquary may preserve:

- artifacts;
- inscriptions;
- monuments;
- glyphs;
- interpretations;
- fragments;
- relics;
- visions;
- records intended for discovery.

---

## Institutional Role

The Reliquary is distinct from other BitPangea institutions:

- **The Archive** preserves historically important materials.
- **The Chronicle** preserves chronology.
- **The Codex** preserves structured inquiry and recognized knowledge.
- **The Foundation** preserves the constitutional library.
- **The Reliquary** preserves discoverable works and hidden records.

The Reliquary should remain a preservation institution rather than becoming a public directory of everything it contains.

---

## Public Threshold

The public Reliquary page serves as a threshold.

It establishes the existence of the preservation layer without disclosing its complete structure, routes, or contents.

The threshold page should remain restrained, atmospheric, and consistent with the principle of discovery.

---

## Repository Guidance

When maintaining this directory:

1. keep public paths stable;
2. preserve earlier significant versions before major revisions;
3. verify all public links, image paths, and metadata;
4. avoid using the README as a complete sitemap;
5. avoid documenting concealed routes, trigger words, or private discovery records;
6. keep public documentation free of Builder-only maintenance notes;
7. document major institutional changes in the appropriate BitPangea journal or Chronicle entry;
8. preserve discoverability without making discovery automatic.

---

## What The Reliquary Is Not

The Reliquary is not:

- a conventional public index;
- a complete sitemap;
- a substitute for The Archive;
- a general media library;
- a repository for unfinished drafts;
- a place where concealed routes are publicly explained.

Its purpose is preservation through discovery.
