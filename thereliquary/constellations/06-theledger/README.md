## Record Role

**The Ledger** is preserved as the sixth Constellation of Digital Antiquity.

Its domain is:

```text
Record
```

It represents:

- Memory
- Permanence
- Accountability

The page frames The Ledger as:

> **The keeper of what must not be forgotten.**

Its preserved role is not merely storage.

It is the conversion of passing events into enduring records capable of being remembered, examined, and used as evidence.

---

## Preservation

The record begins from the problem of transience:

> **Before the Digital World could remember itself, events disappeared when the moment ended.**

The Ledger changed that condition.

The page states that:

- what was written could remain after the writer was gone;
- what was recorded could be examined by someone who had not witnessed it;
- actions could leave evidence;
- agreements could survive memory;
- history no longer depended entirely upon recollection.

The preserved transformation is therefore from momentary event to durable record.

## What The Ledger Does Not Do

The page carefully distinguishes The Ledger from earlier Constellations.

It states:

> **The Ledger did not compute.**

> **It did not carry the message.**

> **It did not reveal the world or search through it.**

Instead:

> **It preserved what the world had done.**

This boundary should remain explicit.

The Ledger is the Constellation of record, not computation, communication, interface, or search.
