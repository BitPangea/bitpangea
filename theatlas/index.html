One unified supercontinent. One finite World. 21,000,000 Parcels.

The Atlas is the canonical design home of ₿itPangea: the place where constitutional truth is translated into World Form, spatial architecture, Parcel structure, cartographic expression, and the enduring design of the World.

BitPangea is constitutionally constrained, but intentionally designed. The Foundation and The Codex establish what must remain true. The Atlas records what now exists, what is now being designed, and how those truths are intentionally expressed as a World.

The Founding Constraint

BitPangea consists of one finite, complete, continuous digital World containing exactly 21,000,000 Parcels. Its Parcel population is fixed. The World does not expand or duplicate, and no central authority may alter that constitutional fact.

The Atlas Structure

The Atlas is not a single map or document. It is the institutional design record through which the World may be understood at several distinct levels. Its principal branches separate deep structural architecture from the designed World, the Parcels that compose its scarce cadastral fabric, and the cartographic systems through which BitPangea is represented and encountered.

Constitution, Design, and Implementation

BitPangea distinguishes constitutional truth from intentional design and technical realization. The Foundation and The Codex constrain what must remain true. The Atlas organizes the design of the World within those truths. Implementation determines how that design is rendered, accessed, computed, and expressed without turning replaceable technology into constitutional law.

The Foundation & The Codex

Establish what must remain true: permanent constraints, constitutional identity, intrinsic structure, and governing invariants.

The Atlas

Records the designed World: architecture, geography, Parcels, cartography, naming, form, and structural expression within constitutional truth.

Implementation

Realizes Atlas design through replaceable technologies, services, interfaces, and systems without becoming the permanent truth they implement.

Atlas Working Principle The deeper the layer, the more permanent it should be. The closer the layer is to the user, the more freedom it should have to evolve.

Principles Guiding The Atlas

  • One finite, complete, continuous digital World
  • Exactly 21,000,000 Parcels
  • The 21,000,000 Parcels do not constitutionally exhaust every cartographically valid portion of The World
  • The complete Parcel population remains one single Contiguity through unbroken paths of Parcel Adjacencies
  • Design remains consistent with The Foundation and The Codex
  • Architecture and visible World expression are distinct design concerns
  • Parcel morphology and foundational spatial order are separate design problems
  • Permanent spatial truth should remain independent of ownership, runtime, and user experience
  • Addressable space need not be materialized as stored objects
  • The globe is an approach and presentation experience; BitPangea is experienced as a flat lived World after cloud entry
  • The Outliers and The Verge remain permissible design space within constitutional constraints
  • Implementation choices remain distinct from constitutional law
  • Survey first. Build later.
  • Substrate ≠ Parcel experience.
  • No central authority may expand or duplicate the map
  • Designed for long-term civilization building
