## Purpose of This Space

Persistence / Provenance exists to preserve continuity across change.

It provides the architectural space through which BitPangea can retain trustworthy records of:

- state;
- history;
- lineage;
- authorship;
- validation;
- succession.

These records may remain meaningful even as implementations, services, institutions, and technologies evolve.

---

## Governing Direction

The source defines the domain’s governing direction as deliberately bounded.

Persistence / Provenance should preserve evidence of:

- what changed;
- what endured;
- where a record came from;
- how canonical continuity was established.

It must do so without allowing persistence or provenance machinery to redefine foundational spatial truth.

The source further states that verification technology may change while the meaning of the records being preserved should remain recoverable and independently understandable.

---

This principle should remain exact.

## Architectural Boundary

## Structural Domain

The source defines Persistence / Provenance as:

It may apply across multiple Architecture domains rather than occupying one fixed position in a universal dependency stack.

## Dependency Standing

The source states that Persistence / Provenance depends upon stable canonical meanings, records, or state defined elsewhere in The Architecture.

It explicitly states that no strict linear position has yet been established between this domain and:

- Identity / Rights / Control;
- Interoperability;
- World Runtime;
- Experience Architecture.

The page directs that these relationships should be resolved individually as those domains mature.

No stricter dependency sequence should be inferred from this page.

---

## Relationship to Foundational Spatial Truth

Persistence / Provenance does not redefine the permanent spatial foundation.

The source explicitly separates historical state and continuity mechanisms from the Foundational Survey Fabric.

Persistence and provenance may preserve records about canonical spatial truth, but they do not become the source of that truth merely by preserving it.

---

## Relationship to Verification

Verification belongs within this domain as part of preserving trustworthy records.

The source does not specify final verification mechanisms.

Those remain open.

It does establish that verification technology itself may change without making the preserved record unintelligible or dependent on one implementation.

---

## Relationship to Canonical Succession

Canonical succession is within the domain’s stated responsibility.

The source does not define the final succession rules.

Those remain open.

The domain’s role is to preserve continuity and the records necessary to understand how canonical continuity was established without turning the persistence machinery itself into foundational spatial authority.
