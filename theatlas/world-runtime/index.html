Runtime

Execution, Synchronization, and Service Architecture

The canonical home of BitPangea’s durable execution and service architecture.

World Runtime is the architectural domain reserved for the execution, synchronization, communication, routing, service, and other operational functions through which BitPangea may act as a live digital World. Its purpose is to keep those functions structurally distinct from permanent spatial truth, Parcel identity, and other enduring architectural records so that runtime systems may evolve without rewriting the World beneath them.

This page establishes the permanent institutional home for that architecture within The Atlas. The domain is intentionally reserved: runtime capabilities are important enough to require a distinct architectural boundary, but no particular execution model, service topology, synchronization mechanism, or operational technology is presented here as established World architecture.

Atlas Working Principle Runtime enables function. It should not become the source of permanent World truth.

Standing

Structural Domain

The architectural domain through which execution, synchronization, communication, routing, shared services, and other live World functions may operate above permanent spatial and cadastral truth.

Status

Reserved as an Atlas architectural domain. Its boundary is preserved so that live operational functions do not become embedded in permanent spatial truth, Parcel identity, or other foundational architecture.

Authority

The Foundation and The Codex constrain what must remain true. The Atlas records the architectural design that gives those truths durable spatial expression.

Purpose of This Space

World Runtime exists to provide an architectural home for the live systems that may make BitPangea responsive, connected, synchronized, and operational. These functions may include execution environments, messaging, event handling, routing, shared services, state propagation, and other active capabilities that allow higher systems to function without becoming part of the permanent spatial substrate.

Its governing direction is deliberately bounded: runtime should enable function without defining permanent place. Operational systems may change, be replaced, distributed, upgraded, or reimplemented while canonical spatial truth, Parcel identity, and durable architectural meaning remain intact. Runtime should depend on the World’s permanent architecture; the permanent architecture should not depend on any one runtime.

Atlas Working Principle Ground establishes place. Runtime enables function. Civilization creates meaning.
