## Architectural Role

The Foundational Survey Fabric preserves stable, implementation-independent spatial truth.

- Parcel identity;
- ownership;
- rendering;
- infrastructure;
- governance;
- civilizational meaning.

Its role is therefore foundational and spatial.

It establishes canonical reference beneath higher architectural domains without redefining the responsibilities of those domains.
