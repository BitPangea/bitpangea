Canonical Frame
& Measurement

Specification Section 02 · Derived from the Requirements Framework

The specification boundary for BitPangea’s native spatial scale, permanent orientation, global coordinate frame, and exact canonical measurement system.

This section translates accepted Foundational Survey Fabric requirements into the specification rules for canonical frame and measurement. It now incorporates the candidate mathematical design selected through Formal Mathematical Decisions FMD-01 through FMD-09 wherever those decisions directly resolve frame, coordinate, origin, orientation, area, or precision questions.

Several measurement obligations remain intentionally open. The candidate mathematics now resolve exact coordinate representation, axis meaning, handedness, positive rotation, origin placement, rational-coordinate polygon area, and precision semantics. They do not yet resolve the canonical angular unit, exact general straight-distance scalar representation, exact general path or boundary-length scalar representation, or arbitrary exact rotation closure. Those remain visible Specification gates.

Specification Principle One World. One foundational frame. Many local perspectives.
Measurement Principle Pang defines scale. Canonical mathematics defines precision.
02.0 · Candidate Mathematics Integration

Frame Mathematics Selected · Measurement Closure Partial

FMD-01 establishes exact reduced rational Pang coordinates. FMD-02 establishes the canonical axis mapping, handedness, and positive rotational convention. FMD-03 establishes the permanent origin. FMD-05 and FMD-06 supply exact polygonal geometry and exact orientation predicates. FMD-09 supplies the precision model.

FMD-04 provides the exact Domain bounds used by the frame but belongs primarily to Specification 01. FMD-07 and FMD-08 govern normalization and serialization elsewhere. None of Decisions 01–09 selects the final canonical angular unit or an exact scalar family sufficient to represent all Euclidean distances and arbitrary path lengths, so those obligations remain open rather than being approximated.

02.1 · Native Linear Scale

Pang

The Foundational Survey Fabric shall use the Pang as BitPangea’s native canonical unit of linear spatial measure.

Pang defines spatial scale. It does not define Parcel identity, Survey-cell identity, display scale, physical-world distance, or ultimate spatial precision. One Pang shall have the same exact canonical meaning everywhere within the Survey Domain.

Under the current candidate precision model, sub-Pang position is represented through exact CRPC fractions rather than through a mandatory hierarchy of named subunits.

02.2 · Canonical Coordinate Scalars

Exact Rational Pang Coordinates

Canonical Survey position coordinates shall use Canonical Rational Pang Coordinates (CRPC). Each coordinate scalar is an exact reduced rational value:

q = n/d Pang, where n ∈ ℤ, d ∈ ℕ+, gcd(|n|,d)=1, and canonical zero is 0/1.

Coordinate exactness does not imply that every derived measurement is rational. General Euclidean distance, arbitrary rotation, and some path-length results may require a broader exact scalar representation, which remains a later formal decision.

02.3 · Native Directional Frame

Pankor · Panvath · Panoris · Panvel

The canonical two-dimensional frame uses:

  • +x = Panoris — East
  • -x = Panvel — West
  • +y = Pankor — North
  • -y = Panvath — South

Canonical coordinate tuples are ordered (x,y). Higher layers may translate, rotate, mirror, rename, or otherwise present direction differently, provided those representations remain deterministically resolvable to the canonical Survey frame.

02.4 · Canonical Origin

One Permanent Zero-Reference

The permanent canonical Survey origin is (0,0).

It is selected as the midpoint of the exact Survey Domain coordinate bounds and serves mathematical reference only. It carries no geographic, civic, cultural, political, economic, or symbolic privilege.

02.5 · Global Coordinate Frame

One Foundational Frame

All authoritative BitPangea spatial location shall resolve to one global canonical coordinate frame defined by the permanent origin, the `(x,y)` tuple order, canonical directional axes, native Pang scale, and exact CRPC coordinate mathematics.

Local, Parcel-relative, regional, dimensional, application-specific, and user-facing coordinate systems may exist above the Foundational Survey Fabric, but they shall not become competing sources of canonical spatial truth.

02.6 · Handedness and Rotation

Right-Handed Frame · Counterclockwise Positive Rotation

The canonical two-dimensional Survey frame is right-handed.

Positive rotation is counterclockwise as viewed from the positive normal to the Survey plane. Mirrored or reversed views are derived representations only and do not redefine canonical orientation.

For valid Points A, B, and C, exact orientation is determined by: orient(A,B,C) = (x₂-x₁)(y₃-y₁) - (y₂-y₁)(x₃-x₁). Positive means left / counterclockwise, negative means right / clockwise, and zero means collinear.

02.7 · Canonical Angular System

One Exact Way to Turn — Unit Still Open

The Foundational Survey Fabric shall define one exact canonical angular system for orientation and rotation relative to the permanent frame.

The orientation baseline is now fixed: zero-direction reference is Panoris / +x and positive rotation is counterclockwise. However, the final canonical angular unit, exact angle representation, and closure rules for arbitrary rotations remain unresolved.

Degrees, headings, local directions, and other presentation conventions may be used above the Foundational Survey Fabric only when they map exactly back to the eventual canonical angular system.

02.8 · Canonical Spatial Separation

Exact Straight Spatial Separation — Scalar Closure Still Open

The Foundational Survey Fabric shall define one exact canonical measure of straight spatial separation expressed in Pangs.

For Points with CRPC coordinates, squared Euclidean separation is exactly representable as: Δx² + Δy². The corresponding distance may require an irrational algebraic value such as a square root of a rational number.

The Specification therefore shall not substitute floating-point approximation for canonical truth. The exact scalar representation for general straight distance remains open pending a dedicated formal decision.

02.9 · Canonical Area

Exact Spatial Measure

Canonical area shall be expressed in square Pangs.

For a valid polygonal SCPE with CRPC vertices, exact signed area is computed by the rational shoelace expression. Absolute area is therefore exactly representable as a rational number of square Pangs.

Canonical area remains geometric only and independent of ownership, use, value, identity, governance, or other higher-layer semantics.

02.10 · Canonical Length

Path and Geometric Boundary Length — General Scalar Closure Still Open

The Foundational Survey Fabric shall define one exact canonical method for measuring the length of paths, lines, boundaries, and other line-like spatial expressions.

Individual Segment length may require an exact square-root scalar even when both endpoints use CRPC coordinates. Summed path or polygon boundary length may therefore require exact expressions beyond rational numbers.

The general exact scalar representation and normalization rules for such lengths remain open. Approximate decimal length is not canonical truth.

02.11 · Address and Measure Agreement

One Spatial Truth

Canonical Survey references and Pang-based position, spatial separation, area, and extent shall remain exactly and reproducibly related.

The current candidate position model uses CRPC coordinates and Exact Coordinate Extension rather than a mandatory hierarchical precision system. A future canonical address grammar must resolve deterministically to that same exact Survey meaning.

02.12 · Transformation and Scale

Transform the Representation, Not the Truth

Derived views may rotate, mirror, translate, zoom, enlarge, compress, or otherwise transform presentation, but no derived transformation may redefine canonical orientation, Pang scale, or authoritative Survey position.

Translation by exact CRPC offsets and uniform scaling by positive rational factors preserve CRPC coordinates. Arbitrary rotation may generate non-rational coordinates and therefore is not yet closed as a canonical-position operation under the current candidate mathematics.

One Pang remains one Pang everywhere, regardless of how a higher-layer view represents it.

Integrated Candidate Mathematics

Pang and square Pang; CRPC coordinates; tuple order (x,y); +x Panoris, -x Panvel, +y Pankor, -y Panvath; origin (0,0); one right-handed global frame; counterclockwise positive rotation; exact orientation determinant; exact rational polygon area; ECEM sub-Pang precision.

Still Open in the Specification

Canonical angular unit and exact angle representation; arbitrary exact rotation closure; exact general straight-distance scalar representation; exact general path and boundary-length scalar representation; and complete transformation-result closure.

Candidate Integration Standing

Frame Core Ready · Measurement System Partially Closed

No contradiction was found between Specification 02 and FMD-01 through FMD-09. The frame, coordinate, origin, orientation, area, and precision components are sufficiently defined for core prototype work and for exact production placement of the selected Spatial Ground World SCPE using rational translation and uniform positive rational scale.

The full measurement system is not yet complete. Canonical adoption of Specification 02 must wait until its still-open angular, distance, path-length, and transformation-closure obligations are formally resolved and the integrated Specification passes Conformance, Reference Vector, independent implementation, adversarial, and adoption review.
