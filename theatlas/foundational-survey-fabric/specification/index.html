Foundational Survey Fabric · Formal Mathematical Structure

The formal structure through which BitPangea will define precisely how the Foundational Survey Fabric works.

The Foundational Survey Fabric Specification is the formal mathematical layer beneath implementation. It will translate the accepted requirements into exact definitions for domain, frame, measurement, addressing, refinement, geometry, operations, serialization, and computability.

The Specification is not yet complete. Findings #1–#85 deliberately established the governing requirements before prematurely selecting much of the final mathematics. These seven sections therefore preserve both what the requirements already establish and what must still be formally resolved, tested, and proven.

Together, these seven sections define the complete intended structure of the Foundational Survey Fabric Specification: from the finite Survey Domain and canonical frame through addressing, geometry, operations, serialization, and the rules that determine whether a spatial result is exact, finite, valid, reproducible, and authoritative.

Several sections presently describe specification boundaries rather than completed mathematics. That is intentional. The Requirements Framework establishes what must be satisfied; the Specification will define the mathematics that satisfies it. Formal choices should be added here only after they survive the accepted requirements, edge cases, independent implementation, and later conformance testing.
