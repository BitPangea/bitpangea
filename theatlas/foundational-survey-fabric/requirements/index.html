Foundational Survey Fabric · Findings #1–#85

The governing requirements that any valid Foundational Survey Fabric must satisfy before formal mathematical design and prototype testing.

The Foundational Survey Fabric Requirements Framework defines what BitPangea’s permanent spatial reference architecture must preserve, permit, exclude, and prove. These requirements establish the architectural envelope within which the later mathematical specification must operate.

The framework does not itself select the final mathematics, addressing scheme, subdivision model, implementation technology, or higher-layer architecture. Instead, it preserves the permanent conditions that any candidate design must satisfy without contradiction.

Atlas Working Principle Do not choose the architecture because it looks elegant. Choose it only after it survives the requirements.

Requirements I

Foundational Purpose and Scope

Defines the minimum permanent purpose of the Foundational Survey Fabric, its separation from Parcels and higher systems, the proper reach of immutability, extension without downward mutation, and preservation of unknown future use.

Requirements VI

Geometry, Topology, Measurement, and Spatial Operations

Defines exact continuity, spatial predicates, transformations, measurement, geometric composition, normalization, serialization, equivalence, precision handling, comparison, connectedness, and other foundational spatial operations.

Requirements VII

Finite Knowability, Computability, and Conformance

Defines finite representability, exact computability, minimal primitives, hidden-state independence, termination, controlled complexity, exact canonical states, the mandatory conformance core, and the readiness gate for mathematical design.

Together, these seven sections preserve the requirements-level definition of the Foundational Survey Fabric as a minimal, exact, finite, planar, semantically neutral, BitPangea-native spatial reference architecture.

The complete framework governs the transition from architectural inquiry into formal specification. No candidate Survey Fabric should advance unless it can satisfy all eighty-five findings while preserving immutable BitPangea spatial truth, exact independent implementation, compatible refinement, and future extensibility.
