Reference Vector Section 04 · Canonical Direction and Rotation

The canonical test set for proving exact agreement on BitPangea’s directional frame, rotation, handedness, and transformed orientation.

Orientation Vectors define the reference cases through which implementations will prove identical interpretation of the native directions Pankor, Panvath, Panoris, and Panvel, together with the canonical rotational convention, frame handedness, and exact transformations of orientation.

The final vector corpus does not yet exist because the exact angular unit, positive rotational convention, and formal transformation mathematics remain unresolved within the Specification. This page therefore establishes the required orientation tests without inventing those canonical values prematurely.

Reference Vector Question Given this exact input, what exact answer must every conforming implementation produce?
04.1 · Pankor Vectors

Canonical Pankor Must Resolve Identically

The vector set shall include canonical orientation cases aligned with Pankor to prove that all conforming implementations interpret the same foundational direction identically.

Higher-layer presentation may translate Pankor as North or display it differently, but the canonical Survey orientation shall remain unchanged.

04.2 · Panvath Vectors

Canonical Panvath Must Resolve Identically

Reference vectors shall include exact cases aligned with Panvath and shall prove the deterministic relationship between Panvath and the rest of the canonical directional frame.

A conforming implementation shall not reinterpret Panvath through local or user-facing orientation conventions.

04.3 · Panoris Vectors

Canonical Panoris Must Resolve Identically

The corpus shall include exact cases aligned with Panoris to prove implementation-independent interpretation of the canonical eastward direction of the Survey frame.

Derived views may rotate or mirror presentation, but canonical Panoris shall remain mathematically fixed.

04.4 · Panvel Vectors

Canonical Panvel Must Resolve Identically

The vector set shall include exact cases aligned with Panvel and shall prove its stable relationship to the canonical global frame.

Panvel shall remain part of one permanent foundational orientation rather than a viewpoint-dependent direction.

04.5 · Opposed-Direction Vectors

Canonical Oppositions Must Be Exact

Reference vectors shall prove the exact mathematical opposition between Pankor and Panvath and between Panoris and Panvel once the formal angular system is selected.

Independent implementations shall derive the same canonical relationship from the same orientation inputs.

04.6 · Canonical Rotation Vectors

One Exact Way to Turn

The corpus shall include exact rotational cases sufficient to prove the canonical angular and rotational rules of the Survey frame.

Once the formal angular unit and positive rotational convention are established, identical rotation inputs shall produce identical canonical orientation results.

04.7 · Full-Cycle Rotation Vectors

Complete Rotation Must Return to Identical Orientation

Reference vectors shall include complete-cycle rotations that must resolve exactly to the original canonical orientation.

No cumulative approximation or implementation-specific drift shall be permitted in canonical rotational truth.

04.8 · Handedness Vectors

The Coordinate Frame Must Have One Mathematical Handedness

The vector set shall include orientation cases sufficient to prove the permanent handedness of the two-dimensional canonical frame once that convention is formally selected.

Mirrored presentation may exist as a derived transformation, but it shall not redefine the canonical handedness.

04.9 · Mirrored-View Vectors

Reflection Must Remain a Derived Transformation

Reference vectors shall demonstrate how reflected or mirrored orientation is mapped exactly back to the unchanged canonical frame.

A mirrored view shall not create a second foundational orientation or competing directional truth.

04.10 · Rotated-View Vectors

Presentation Rotation Must Resolve Back to Canonical Direction

The corpus shall include derived local or presentation rotations to prove that transformed orientation can be deterministically mapped back to Pankor, Panvath, Panoris, Panvel, and the canonical rotational frame.

Transform the view, not the truth.

04.11 · Combined Transformation Vectors

Rotation and Reflection Must Compose Deterministically

Where the Specification supports composed orientation transformations, vectors shall test combinations of rotation, reflection, translation of frame origin where applicable, and inverse transformation.

Every valid composition shall resolve to one exact canonical orientation result.

04.12 · Cross-Implementation Orientation Agreement

Every Implementation Must Face the Same Canonical Direction

Independent conforming implementations shall return identical canonical directional, rotational, and handedness results for identical inputs under the same governing Specification.

A disagreement indicates an implementation defect, specification ambiguity, version incompatibility, or unresolved mathematical dependency requiring correction.

Established by Requirements and Specification Scope

BitPangea has one permanent canonical directional frame using Pankor, Panvath, Panoris, and Panvel; one global coordinate frame; one canonical angular system must eventually be defined; and handedness, rotation, mirroring, and transformed views must remain exact and deterministically resolvable to the singular foundational orientation.

Canonical Vector Corpus Not Yet Possessed

Actual angular values, positive rotation convention, exact handedness declaration, rotation encodings, transformation matrices or equivalent formalism, and authoritative expected outputs cannot be defined until the final angular and transformation mathematics are selected and tested.
