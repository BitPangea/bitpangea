Reference Vector Section 03 · Canonical Spatial Measurement

The canonical test set for proving exact agreement on Pang-based distance, area, path and boundary length, and sub-Pang precision.

Pang Measurement Vectors define the reference cases through which implementations will prove identical canonical measurement of spatial separation, area, path length, boundary length, and refinement below one Pang.

The final vector corpus does not yet exist because the exact distance, area, length, angular, and refinement mathematics remain unresolved within the Specification. This page therefore establishes the measurement cases that must eventually be tested without inventing formulas or expected values prematurely.

Reference Vector Question Given this exact input, what exact answer must every conforming implementation produce?
03.1 · Pang Unit Vectors

One Pang Must Mean the Same Thing Everywhere

The vector set shall include canonical cases that demonstrate the exact linear meaning of one Pang within the global Survey frame.

Conforming implementations shall reproduce the same canonical measure regardless of location within the Survey Domain.

03.2 · Canonical Separation Vectors

Exact Distance Between Canonical Positions

Reference vectors shall include pairs of canonical positions whose exact spatial separation must be measured identically by every conforming implementation.

These cases shall test the canonical distance mathematics only and shall not imply routing, travel time, network cost, or experiential distance.

03.3 · Zero-Separation Vectors

Equivalent Position Must Measure Zero Separation

The corpus shall include differently expressed but canonically equivalent positions where applicable to prove that equivalent canonical location produces exactly zero spatial separation.

Any nonzero result for canonically identical position shall constitute a conformance failure.

03.4 · Square Pang Area Vectors

Canonical Area Must Be Exact

The vector set shall include exact spatial extents whose area can be expressed canonically in square Pangs once the formal area mathematics are selected.

Independent implementations shall produce identical authoritative area results for identical extents.

03.5 · Composite Area Vectors

Composition Must Preserve Exact Measure

Reference vectors shall include unions, differences, intersections, or other exact compositions where area can be tested across derived spatial extents.

These cases shall help prove consistency between geometric composition and canonical area measurement.

03.6 · Path Length Vectors

Length Is Distinct From Separation

The corpus shall include exact line-like or path-like spatial expressions whose canonical length must be measured independently from the straight-line separation between endpoints.

The expected result shall test geometric path length only and shall not assign semantic meaning to the path.

03.7 · Boundary Length Vectors

Exact Perimeter-Like Measure Without Higher-Layer Meaning

Where canonical extents possess measurable boundaries, reference vectors shall test exact boundary length according to the governing Survey mathematics.

The Survey result shall remain geometric only and shall not imply Parcel perimeter, legal boundary, or other higher-layer interpretation.

03.8 · Sub-Pang Precision Vectors

Refinement Must Support Exact Measure Below One Pang

The vector set shall include positions, separations, lengths, and extents smaller than one Pang to prove exact sub-Pang measurement through the canonical refinement system.

These cases shall not require permanently named subunits unless the final Specification later establishes them.

03.9 · Mixed-Precision Vectors

Different Refinement Depths Must Resolve Exactly

Reference vectors shall include calculations involving canonical inputs expressed at different supported refinement depths.

Every conforming implementation shall resolve the operation without silently discarding precision or redefining the Pang scale.

03.10 · Lossless Measurement Conversion Vectors

Equivalent Precision Must Remain Equivalent

Where canonical measurement may be expressed through different exact representations, the vector corpus shall demonstrate lossless conversion and identical canonical meaning.

Lossless transformation shall preserve the exact authoritative measurement.

03.11 · Lossy Measurement Reduction Vectors

Reduced Precision Must Be Detectable

The corpus shall include measurement representations whose precision has been truncated, rounded, generalized, or otherwise reduced.

Conforming implementations shall distinguish those derived approximations from complete canonical measurement rather than treating them as exactly equivalent.

03.12 · Cross-Implementation Measurement Agreement

Every Implementation Must Return the Same Measure

Independent conforming implementations shall produce identical canonical separation, area, path length, boundary length, and sub-Pang results from identical canonical inputs.

A mismatch indicates an implementation defect, specification ambiguity, version incompatibility, or unresolved mathematical dependency requiring correction.

Established by Requirements and Specification Scope

Pang is BitPangea’s native canonical unit of linear measure; square Pangs measure area; canonical spatial separation, area, path length, and boundary length must be exact; Pang scale must remain uniform across the Survey Domain; and sub-Pang precision must be supplied through exact refinement mathematics.

Canonical Vector Corpus Not Yet Possessed

Actual measurement inputs, exact expected Pang values, square-Pang results, path and boundary formulas, sub-Pang examples, mixed-precision fixtures, and canonical machine-readable outputs cannot be defined until the final measurement and refinement mathematics are selected and tested.
