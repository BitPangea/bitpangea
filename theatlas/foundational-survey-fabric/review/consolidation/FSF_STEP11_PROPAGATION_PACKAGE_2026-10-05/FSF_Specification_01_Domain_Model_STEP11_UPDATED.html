Specification Section 01 · Derived from the Requirements Framework

The specification boundary for BitPangea’s finite canonical Survey Domain and its permanent two-dimensional ground model.

This section translates accepted Foundational Survey Fabric requirements into the specification rules for the Survey Domain and Ground Model. It preserves the Requirements-level commitments to a finite, connected, two-dimensional canonical reference domain while incorporating the candidate mathematical design selected through Formal Mathematical Decisions FMD-01 through FMD-09 where those decisions directly govern Domain semantics.

The integrated candidate now selects an exact mathematical Domain geometry, exact closed-boundary semantics, and an exact parameterized Domain-validity rule. FMD-04B now resolves numerical Survey Domain capacity at H = 1,000,000 Pang and establishes the governed production placement envelope [-500,000,+500,000]². These mathematical selections remain candidate Specification content pending completion of the remaining institutional identity / adoption gate.

Specification Principle Reference capacity is not territory.
01.0 · Candidate Mathematics Integration

Selected Mathematics, Not Yet Adopted Canon

This section now integrates the Domain-relevant results of the candidate FSF mathematical design. In particular, FMD-01 supplies exact reduced rational Pang coordinates; FMD-03 supplies the permanent origin rule; and FMD-04 supplies the candidate Domain geometry, closed-boundary convention, exact numerical half-span H = 1,000,000 Pang, and governed production placement envelope.

FMD-02, FMD-05 through FMD-08, and FMD-09 remain compatible with this section but primarily govern the frame, primitive geometry, predicates, normalization, serialization, and precision semantics specified elsewhere. Their relevant consequences are reflected here only where needed to define Domain validity and preserve the boundary between foundational reference capacity and higher-layer meaning.

01.1 · Canonical Survey Domain

Finite Reference Domain

The Foundational Survey Fabric shall define one finite canonical Survey Domain. The Domain establishes the exact scope within which canonical BitPangea spatial reference is valid.

Under the current candidate mathematical design, the Survey Domain is one closed, axis-aligned square centered on the permanent Survey origin and expressed through exact CRPC Pang coordinates.

The Survey Domain is a reference architecture, not a cartographic declaration of World membership. A location may be validly referenceable within the Survey Domain without thereby becoming BitPangea World-space.

01.2 · Dimensional Model

Two-Dimensional Planar Ground

The canonical Survey Domain shall be modeled as a two-dimensional planar spatial ground. Vertical, volumetric, immersive, nested, terrain, elevation, and other higher-dimensional or surface expressions belong above the Foundational Survey Fabric.

The Survey Fabric therefore anchors permanent ground position without becoming the visible, geographic, architectural, rendered, or experiential surface of BitPangea.

01.3 · Domain Coherence

One Connected Closed Reference Space

The canonical Survey Domain shall be one connected and internally coherent reference domain rather than a collection of disconnected sectors or a domain containing arbitrary internal holes where canonical reference is unavailable.

Under the current candidate mathematics, the Domain is a single closed square and is therefore connected, path-connected, bounded, and hole-free as a foundational reference extent.

Higher spatial layers may designate portions of that reference capacity differently without requiring corresponding gaps in the foundational reference system.

01.4 · Exact Domain Geometry and Limits

Closed Square · Exact Pang Bounds

Under the candidate mathematical design, the canonical Survey Domain is expressed parametrically as:

D = [-1,000,000,+1,000,000] × [-1,000,000,+1,000,000], with canonical H = 1,000,000 Pang.

The Domain therefore has exact width 2,000,000 Pang, exact height 2,000,000 Pang, and exact area 4,000,000,000,000 square Pang. Its four boundary edges and four corner points are included in valid Survey space.

The square geometry, closed boundary, and numerical capacity are candidate-resolved under FMD-04. The canonical half-span is H = 1,000,000 Pang. The earlier use of the same number remains historically superseded as to rationale; Day #131 independently reselects it as a governed foundational design constant.

These limits determine where valid canonical Survey reference ends once H is selected. They shall not themselves be treated as BitPangea’s World boundary, Extent expression, Verge, coastline, Exterior, or other cartographic feature.

01.5 · Exact Domain Validity

Valid Survey Position or Invalid Reference

Let a candidate canonical Point be p = (x,y), with both coordinates expressed as valid normalized CRPC Pang values.

The Point belongs to the Survey Domain exactly when: -1,000,000 ≤ x ≤ +1,000,000 and -1,000,000 ≤ y ≤ +1,000,000.

The comparison is exact. No tolerance, epsilon, snapping, rounding, or nearest-valid-position rule is permitted. A Point outside either bound is invalid as a canonical Survey Point rather than merely classified as non-World.

Concrete numerical edge cases SHALL use the adopted Domain extrema ±1,000,000 Pang.

01.6 · World-Space Separation

Reference Capacity and World Membership

World membership shall be established by higher spatial Architecture domains using valid canonical Survey Fabric references. The Foundational Survey Fabric determines where canonical reference is possible; higher spatial domains determine what portions of that reference capacity constitute The World.

The Survey Domain is therefore not required to geometrically match BitPangea’s irregular supercontinental World Form. The production Spatial Ground instance SHALL lie strictly inside the governed placement envelope P = [-500,000,+500,000]², itself strictly inside the Survey Domain. This placement rule shall not redefine the foundational meaning of the Domain itself.

01.7 · Higher-Layer Geometry

No Required Alignment to Survey Subdivision

Higher-layer spatial entities shall not be required to align their authoritative geometry with Survey Fabric subdivisions, grid lines, tiles, cells, or indexing structures merely because such derived structures may exist.

The current candidate precision model uses exact coordinate extension rather than a mandatory hierarchical grid or parent/child Point system. The Survey Fabric provides reference. Higher layers remain responsible for shape.

01.8 · Center and Internal Partitions

Mathematical Origin Without Semantic Privilege

The permanent canonical origin is (0,0), the midpoint of the symmetric parameterized Domain bounds [-H,+H] on both axes. It is mathematically privileged as the zero-reference of the Survey frame and nothing more.

The Survey Domain shall not establish a privileged civic, cultural, geographic, political, economic, or institutional center. Internal sectors, quadrants, regions, zones, grids, or partitions used for indexing, computation, precision, or serialization shall likewise remain semantically neutral at the foundational level.

Integrated Candidate Mathematics

Finite two-dimensional Domain; one connected closed axis-aligned square; exact parameterized CRPC Pang bounds [-H,+H]; permanent origin at (0,0); exact boundary inclusion; exact parameterized Domain-validity comparisons; separation of Survey validity from World membership; no mandatory grid alignment; no foundational semantic privilege for origin or internal partitions.

Still Outside This Section / Still Open

Final canonical address grammar; angular representation; exact general distance and path-length scalar closure; arbitrary exact rotation and transformation closure; general Boolean/composition closure; operation-output closure; Specification identity details; governed complexity limits; and formal adoption remain outside this section or still unresolved at full-FSF scope.
