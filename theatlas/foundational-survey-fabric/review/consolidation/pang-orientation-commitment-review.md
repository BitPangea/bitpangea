Before Foundational Survey Fabric closeout, BitPangea must distinguish:

1. what has already been intentionally committed at the Requirements level; and

- Survey Domain geometry;

- origin placement;
- coordinate representation;
- handedness choice;
- positive rotational sign;
- angular unit;
- angular notation;

- addressing grammar;
- serialization.

It asks only:

> **Which measurement and orientation commitments are already permanent constraints on any future Foundational Survey Fabric mathematics?**

# IV. Pang

## Commitment

Pang is intentionally permanent as the native canonical linear spatial measure of BitPangea.

This is not merely a placeholder name.

The future mathematical design must preserve the principle:

> **Pang defines scale. Canonical mathematics defines precision.**

Pang is not:

- a Parcel;
- a survey-cell identity;
- a display pixel;
- a physical-world meter or foot;
- the smallest possible spatial unit;
- the final precision level.

The Pang gives BitPangea its own native spatial scale.

### Confirmed Standing

**PANG — PERMANENT REQUIREMENTS-LEVEL COMMITMENT**

### Still Open

- exact coordinate representation in relation to Pang;
- exact domain dimensions in Pangs;
- exact sub-Pang precision method;
- exact distance mathematics;
- exact line / boundary length mathematics;
- serialization and notation.

# V. Square Pang

## Commitment

Canonical spatial area is intentionally expressed in **square Pangs**.

This establishes a native BitPangea area expression without importing terrestrial area systems into the foundational layer.

Square Pang is not:

- a Parcel-area requirement;
- a claim that Parcels must be square;
- a tessellation unit;
- a statement about equal Parcel area;
- a property of ownership or value.

It is the canonical dimensional expression of area under Pang scale.

### Confirmed Standing

**SQUARE PANG — PERMANENT REQUIREMENTS-LEVEL COMMITMENT**

### Still Open

- exact area algorithm;
- exact geometric primitive model;
- exact representation of extents;
- relationship between area mathematics and any later Parcel geometry.

# VI. Uniform Pang Scale

## Commitment

One Pang must represent the same exact canonical linear measure everywhere within the Survey Domain.

No canonical location may possess a different Pang scale merely because of:

- geography;
- rendering;
- projection;
- Region;
- local frame;
- experience;
- display scale.

Higher layers may visually stretch, compress, zoom, or transform representations.

They may not change the canonical Pang.

### Confirmed Standing

**UNIFORM PANG SCALE — PERMANENT REQUIREMENTS-LEVEL COMMITMENT**

### Still Open

The mathematics by which uniform scale is represented.

# VIII. One Permanent Canonical Origin

## Commitment

The Foundational Survey Fabric must possess one permanent mathematical zero-reference.

The commitment is to the **existence, uniqueness, permanence, and mathematical role** of the origin.

The commitment is **not** to its present placement.

The Requirements explicitly preserve the eventual placement for later mathematical design.

The origin must not be chosen merely because a location is:

- a Parcel;
- Genesis Parcel;
- a landmark;
- a settlement;
- a capital;
- the World’s geographic center;
- a cultural center;
- symbolically important.

Its placement should be chosen for:

- mathematical clarity;
- determinism;
- long-term stability.

### Confirmed Standing

**ONE PERMANENT CANONICAL ORIGIN — PERMANENT REQUIREMENTS-LEVEL COMMITMENT**

### Still Open

**EXACT ORIGIN PLACEMENT — DELIBERATELY UNRESOLVED**

This is a critical distinction.

The existence of one origin is established.

Where that origin is placed is not.

- exact positive rotational direction;
- exact angular unit and notation;

- translated units;
- degrees or headings for display;
- rotated or mirrored views;
- user-facing direction terminology;
- experiential scale.
