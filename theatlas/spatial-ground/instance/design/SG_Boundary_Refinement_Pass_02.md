- Northwestern Reach;
- western outer edge;

## 5. Character Added

Pass 02 intentionally avoids uniform edge complexity.

### Northwestern Reach

The western and northwestern edge now carries restrained alternating articulation.

This makes Lobe A less generic while preserving its broad stabilizing mass.

### Northern Shoulder

Small asymmetrical shifts interrupt the overly smooth transition across the top of the central body.

### Southeastern Reach

Lobe C now carries more local edge rhythm, helping it read as a distinct major form rather than merely a continuation of the eastern outline.

### Southwestern Reach

The lower-western edge receives sparse, quieter articulation appropriate to its role as an anchor mass.

### Principal Bays and Waist

These remain more controlled.

Their identity already comes from negative-space geometry, so they were refined conservatively rather than made noisy.
