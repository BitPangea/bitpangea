## 8. Silhouette Judgment

At this first refinement scale, the candidate succeeds as a **structural silhouette**, but it should not yet be treated as aesthetically final.

What is already working:

- the World reads as one mass;
- asymmetry is unmistakable;
- Lobe B is strongly distinctive;
- Principal Bay 1 produces a memorable eastern incision;
- Lobe C produces the intended southeast continuation;
- the lower-left mass stabilizes the composition;
- no literal symbol dominates the form.

What still needs refinement:

- broad outer arcs remain too mechanically smooth in places;
- some transitions between major forms are still visibly derived from a small scaffold;
- Lobe A needs more individual character;
- the western edge needs one or two memorable secondary articulations;
- Lobe C can gain greater local identity without adding fragmentation;
- local edge rhythm should become less uniform.

The correct response is **refinement**, not macroform redesign.
