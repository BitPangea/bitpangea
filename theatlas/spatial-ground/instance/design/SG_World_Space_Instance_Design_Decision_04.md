## 1. Decision

The first actual geometric skeleton of BitPangea SHALL use a **27-anchor ordered macro-boundary scaffold** in the non-canonical Normalized Design Frame.

The scaffold realizes the already-selected form:

- one central body;
- four attached lobes;
- two principal bays;
- one narrow connective waist;
- strong asymmetry;
- approximately 1.30:1 horizontal dominance;
- NW→SE compositional energy;
- no Ground holes;
- no disconnected Ground islands.

The scaffold is the first finite geometric statement of the BitPangea silhouette.

---

## 2. Design Frame

The scaffold uses the temporary design frame:

```text
x ∈ [-0.95, +0.95]
y ∈ [-0.73, +0.73]
```

which yields a bounding aspect of approximately:

```text
1.30137 : 1
```

This frame is **not**:

- FSF;
- Survey space;
- canonical Ground coordinates;
- a second coordinate system for BitPangea.

It is a Creator design workspace only.

---

## 3. Boundary Order

Anchors are traversed clockwise from the North Extreme:

```text
A00 → A01 → ... → A26 → A00
```

The straight chords between the current anchors are diagnostic scaffold edges.

They are **not yet the final FEBCM boundary**.

Future refinement will add vertices between selected anchors without changing the macro-composition.

## 5. Extreme Points

The scaffold establishes the first design-frame extremes:

```text
North: A00 = (-0.10, +0.73)
South: A14 = (+0.30, -0.73)
East:  A05/A11 = (+0.95, +0.48 / -0.20)
West:  A22 = (-0.95, +0.12)
```

This asymmetry is intentional.

The northern and southern extremes are horizontally displaced, strengthening the NW→SE compositional diagonal.

---

## 6. Lobe A — Northwestern Reach

Lobe A is defined principally by:

```text
A23  West Tip
A24  Northwest Tip
A25  Upper Shoulder
```

with support from:

```text
A22  West Extreme
A26  Central NW Shoulder
```

Design character:

- broad;
- stable;
- substantial;
- less elongated than Lobe B;
- visually capable of balancing the southeastern mass.

---

## 7. Lobe B — Northeastern Reach

Lobe B is defined by:

```text
A03  Upper Shoulder
A04  NE Tip
A05  East Tip
A06  Lower Shoulder / Bay 1 North Mouth
```

It attaches to the central body through:

```text
A02  Waist Upper
A07  Waist Lower
```

Lobe B is deliberately the most directionally energetic reach.

---

## 8. The Narrow Waist

The first geometric definition of the near-separation is:

```text
A02 = (+0.36, +0.56)
A07 = (+0.40, +0.35)
```

The current design-frame separation is approximately:

```text
0.214 units
```

This is intentionally narrow relative to the World height.

It is not yet a canonical width.

The final geometry must preserve obvious connection while maintaining visual tension.

Principal Bay 1 is the major eastern incision below Lobe B.

```text

South mouth: A09 = (+0.72, +0.10)
```

This produces the deepest major macro-concavity in the candidate silhouette.

Its role is both geographic articulation and negative-space recognition.

## 11. Principal Bay 2

## 12. Lobe D — Southwestern Reach

Lobe D is defined by:

```text
A18  South Shoulder
A19  Southwest Tip
A20  West-Southwest Extreme
A21  Upper Shoulder
```

It is shorter and heavier than B or C.

Its purpose is to anchor the lower-left composition and prevent the eastern reaches from visually overwhelming the World.

---

## 13. Central Body

The central body is not a separate polygon.

It is the broad World mass held between the four lobe attachment systems.

Its upper structure is especially supported by:

```text
A26  Central NW Shoulder
A00  North Extreme
A01  Central NE Shoulder
```

and its eastern constriction by:

```text
A02 / A07
```

The central body remains visually larger than any single lobe.

The scaffold is not:

- canonical FSF geometry;
- final boundary vertices;
- Parcel geometry;
- geography;
- terrain;

- an ownership map.

They are not World place names.

---

## 17. Refinement Discipline

Future intermediate vertices MAY move the local boundary between anchors.

They SHALL NOT casually move the macro anchors themselves.

A macro anchor may be revised only where silhouette testing demonstrates a clear improvement and the revision is recorded.

This prevents detailed refinement from gradually erasing the selected macro-composition.
