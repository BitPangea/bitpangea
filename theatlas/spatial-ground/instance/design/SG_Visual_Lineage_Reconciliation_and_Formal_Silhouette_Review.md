## 2. Visual Lineage Decision

The established BitPangea silhouette image is selected as the **primary visual-lineage reference** for the canonical World macroform.

This does **not** mean the image pixels are canonical Ground.

It means:

> **the recognizable macro-boundary carried by the established artwork is the design lineage from which the exact Ground boundary should be derived.**

Terrain, luminosity, mountains, internal pathways, surface texture, and local coastline noise remain higher-layer or rendering material.

Only the macro-silhouette is relevant here.

---

## 3. Supersession

The earlier abstract geometry remains historically useful but is superseded as the active silhouette lineage.

- the earlier abstract portrait arrangement;

## 6. Formal Silhouette Review

### Test 1 — One-World Test

**PASS**

The silhouette reads as one continuous supercontinental whole.

The large bays and narrow connections create tension without breaking unity.

---

### Test 2 — Recognition Test

**PASS**

The silhouette is unusually distinctive.

Its recognition comes from the relationship between:

- the broad upper mass;
- the pronounced central/eastern negative space;
- the hooked eastern extension;
- the irregular western mass;
- the substantial lower body;
- the narrow connective transitions.

It does not require internal detail to remain identifiable.

---

### Test 3 — Asymmetry Test

**PASS**

The form strongly rejects bilateral, radial, and emblematic symmetry.

No major feature is mirrored.

---

### Test 4 — Compositional Balance Test

**PASS**

The silhouette is asymmetric without appearing accidentally unbalanced.

The upper reach, western mass, eastern hook, central void, and lower mass create counterweight through mass and negative space rather than symmetry.

---

### Test 5 — Articulation Test

**PASS**

The silhouette contains multiple major bays, projections, necks, and recesses.

The articulation is strong enough to create identity but still subordinate to one-World legibility.

---

### Test 6 — No-Fragmentation Test

**PASS**

The major reaches remain attached.

No detached World-space island is required by the macroform.

---

### Test 7 — No-Hole Test

**PASS AT MACROFORM LEVEL**

The large black negative spaces visible in the artwork are interpreted as exterior-connected bays/inlets rather than enclosed Ground-level holes.

The exact FEBCM candidate must continue to preserve this property during final geometry preparation.

---

### Test 8 — Closed-Limit Compatibility

**PASS IN DESIGN**

Nothing in the macroform requires an open-boundary interpretation.

The final FSF region can be represented as a closed simple polygonal region.

---

### Test 9 — Small-Scale Recognition

**PASS**

The primary identity survives substantial simplification.

The strongest features are macro-geometric rather than texture-dependent.

---

### Test 10 — Globe-Distance Recognition

**PASS**

The silhouette has sufficient mass contrast and negative-space identity to remain meaningful at planetary viewing distance.

This is especially compatible with the established Progressive Revelation direction.

---

### Test 11 — Higher-Layer Freedom

**PASS**

The macro-boundary does not dictate:

- terrain;
- Regions;
- roads;
- ownership;
- Parcel organization;
- settlement;
- experience design.

The interior remains highly available to higher architecture.

---

### Test 12 — Digital-Age Identity

**PASS**

The form does not resemble a direct Earth reconstruction or literal technological symbol.

Its identity comes from unfamiliar but coherent macroform.

---
