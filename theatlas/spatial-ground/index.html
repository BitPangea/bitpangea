Canonical Ground and World-Space Architecture

The canonical architectural home of BitPangea World-space membership.

Spatial Ground canonically expresses and preserves which canonical Survey space is BitPangea World-space. It is the Atlas domain that adds canonical World membership to the reference and mathematics supplied by the Foundational Survey Fabric while remaining deliberately silent about the higher meanings built above that membership.

Spatial Ground does not create The World, redefine constitutional truth, or duplicate the Survey Fabric. Constitutional reality constrains the domain; the Creator designs the exact permissible spatial expression where the Constitution leaves room; a recorded Atlas adoption act makes one definition canonical; and Spatial Ground thereafter preserves that adopted World-space for every higher layer that depends upon it.

Atlas Working Principle The Foundational Survey Fabric supplies canonical spatial reference and mathematics. Spatial Ground adds canonical participation in The World.

Standing

Structural Domain

Spatial Ground is the Atlas domain that canonically expresses and preserves World membership over canonical Survey space. It consumes reference and spatial mathematics from the Foundational Survey Fabric and provides stable membership truth upon which higher spatial interpretation may depend.

Status

The Spatial Ground Requirements are drafted and in Adoption Review. Spatial meaning, layer ownership, institutional authority, and consolidation architecture are resolved. The formal membership model remains intentionally deferred to the Specification.

Authority

Constitutional truth constrains. The Creator designs within what remains open. A recorded Atlas Adoption Act makes a permissible definition canonical. Spatial Ground then preserves that adopted World-space without acquiring authority to redefine constitutional truth.

Purpose and Boundary

Spatial Ground exists so that every valid Survey location can receive one exact canonical answer to the question of participation in The World. The answer is total, two-valued, deterministic, independent of later interpretation, and permanent once the canonical World-space instance is adopted.

The domain owns World-membership meaning and nothing beyond it. It does not determine Parcel identity or eligibility, terrain, visual World Form, Regions, Clusters, ownership, rights, settlement, construction, economics, culture, experience, or any other higher-layer interpretation. Those systems may depend on Spatial Ground, but they may not complete, modify, or replace its membership truth.

Architectural Boundary Spatial Ground answers whether canonical Survey space participates in The World. Higher layers determine what that World-space means.

Canonical Scope

Spatial Ground operates over canonical Survey space and preserves a single World-membership truth. The canonical definition may use Survey geometry, topology, scale, precision, refinement, and other Survey-owned mathematics, but Spatial Ground does not redefine or duplicate those foundations.

World-space may coincide in extent with the Survey Domain but is never defined merely as the Survey Domain. Non-World-space is the relational complement of World-space within the operative Survey Domain and receives no independent intrinsic meaning from Spatial Ground.

The canonical limit is a consequence of membership. It may coincide with a recognizable geographic or experiential edge, but coincidence does not make those things identical. Visual form and experiential geography remain higher-layer concerns.

What Spatial Ground Does Not Own

Spatial Ground does not own Parcel truth, higher-layer eligibility, visual World Form, terrain, Regions, Clusters, infrastructure meaning, ownership, rights, settlement, experience, or cultural and economic interpretation. It also does not own Survey reference, coordinates, geometry, topology, scale, measurement, precision, or refinement mechanics.

This narrow responsibility is intentional. Lower layers preserve smaller and more permanent truths; higher layers may add meaning without acquiring authority to alter the World membership beneath them.
