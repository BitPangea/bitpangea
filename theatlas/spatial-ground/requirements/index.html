Spatial Ground · Adopted September 23, 2026

The governing requirements that define how canonical Survey space becomes canonically recognized as BitPangea World-space without absorbing the mathematics below or the meaning above.

The Spatial Ground Requirements Framework defines what BitPangea’s World-membership architecture must preserve, permit, exclude, and prove. Spatial Ground canonically expresses and preserves which canonical Survey space is BitPangea World-space.

These Requirements do not select the formal membership model, redefine the Foundational Survey Fabric, design General Spatial Interpretation, parcelize The World, or establish higher-layer meaning. They preserve the permanent architectural envelope within which the later Spatial Ground Specification must operate.
