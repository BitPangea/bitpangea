# Spatial Ground — Stewardship and Succession Package

**Status:** COMPLETE  
**Creator Period:** Day #122 · September 26, 2026

This package contains the governance instruments required to preserve Spatial Ground through institutional and personnel change without allowing custody or succession to become a source of spatial meaning.

## Files

1. `SG_Stewardship_Instrument.md`
   - defines stewardship as custody and preservation without semantic authority;
   - defines Custodian in Fact;
   - permits multiple stewards;
   - separates preservation from adoption and editorial governance.

2. `SG_Steward_Recognition_Record_Template.md`
   - standard recorded form for recognizing a Steward;
   - records acceptance of preservation duty;
   - explicitly confers no semantic authority.

3. `SG_Succession_Instrument.md`
   - defines the post-Creator `Atlas Adoption Authority — Successor Office`;
   - transfers continuing office powers;
   - explicitly does not transfer exhausted World-space design power after canonical instance adoption;
   - does not confer open-ended curative design authority.

## Architectural Rule

> **Stewardship preserves canon. Succession preserves lawful office. Neither recreates the power to redesign adopted World-space.**
