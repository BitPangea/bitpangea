## 1. Purpose

This glossary preserves the terminology distinctions required for Spatial Ground drafting, adoption, Specification work, Conformance, Preservation, Governance, and downstream framework records.

Its purpose is to prevent terminology drift from creating architectural drift.

A term owned by another BitPangea institution or layer is **used** here and never redefined here.

The ownership convention is:

- **Codex** — The Codex or The Lexicon, as constitutional sources;
- **FSF** — the Foundational Survey Fabric;
- **Ground** — Spatial Ground and its Requirements;
- **Atlas** — Atlas governance and architectural records;
- **Shared** — Atlas shared architectural doctrine;
- **Higher layers** — General Spatial Interpretation, Parcel Cadastre, experience/runtime domains, or other architecture above Spatial Ground;
- **Implementers** — technical systems that realize adopted architecture.

# 2. Space and Membership

## The World

**Meaning:** BitPangea's one World, as constitutionally recognized.

**Owner:** Codex / constitutional sources.

**Do not use as:** an architectural redefinition created by Spatial Ground.

---

## World-space

**Meaning:** The canonical Survey space that participates in The World.

**Owner:** Spatial Ground.

**Preferred use:** the exact canonical subject of the World / non-World membership distinction.

**Do not use as synonyms:** land, terrain, the map, the continent.

Those words may appear in higher-layer interpretation or presentation, but they are not canonical Ground synonyms.

---

## Survey Domain

**Meaning:** The finite domain of valid Survey reference.

**Owner:** Foundational Survey Fabric.

**Do not use as:** a synonym for World-space.

World-space may coincide in extent with the Survey Domain without becoming definitionally identical to it.

---

## Survey reference

**Meaning:** A canonical reference into Survey space.

**Owner:** Foundational Survey Fabric.

**Do not use:** “Ground reference” as if Spatial Ground possessed an independent spatial-reference regime.

---

## membership / World membership

**Meaning:** The two-valued answer to whether a valid Survey location is World-space.

**Owner:** Spatial Ground.

**Canonical values:**

- World-space;
- non-World-space.

**Do not use:** degrees of membership, probabilistic membership, partial membership, mixed membership as a location-level state.

A mixed area or extent outcome is derived and never becomes a third membership value.

---

## non-World-space

**Meaning:** Valid Survey space that does not satisfy the canonical World-membership condition.

**Owner:** Spatial Ground.

**Do not use as canonical synonyms:** outside, void, ocean, frontier, exterior.

Those terms may acquire higher-layer meaning later, but Ground assigns none of them intrinsically.

---

## Extent

**Meaning:** The constitutional property describing the spatial scope of The World.

**Owner:** Codex / World Properties.

**Do not use as:** the Ground membership set, the Ground limit, or a derived spatial measure.

Extent constrains Spatial Ground but does not substitute for the canonical membership definition.

# 3. The Definition and Its Records

## canonical definition

**Meaning:** The adopted determination of World-space membership, considered as meaning.

**Owner:** Spatial Ground.

**Do not use as:** the rule set itself. The governing rule set belongs to the Spatial Ground Specification.

---

## canonical instance

**Meaning:** The canonical definition when contrasting BitPangea's one adopted definition with synthetic, test, or generic instances.

**Owner:** Spatial Ground.

**Use sparingly:** it is not a general synonym for canonical definition.

---

## primary artifact

**Meaning:** The record declared primary for a semantic element.

**Owner:** Atlas governance.

**Do not use:** “the canonical file” merely because a file is published, widely used, or technically authoritative.

Primary status must be declared.

---

## representation

**Meaning:** Any encoding or artifact that carries the canonical definition or is derived from it.

**Owner:** Spatial Ground, subject to Atlas authority over record designation.

**Do not use:** “Ground expression” for an encoding.

Reserve **express / expresses** for the architectural function of Spatial Ground.

---

## express / expresses

**Meaning:** What Spatial Ground does: gives exact, durable, canonical form to constitutional constraint and to the adopted Creator-period spatial design.

**Owner:** Spatial Ground.

**Preferred governing wording:**

> Spatial Ground canonically expresses and preserves which canonical Survey space is BitPangea World-space.

**Do not use for the layer's function:** establishes, creates, defines.

---

## validity — Ground definition

**Meaning:** Satisfaction of every condition a canonical Spatial Ground definition must meet.

**Owner:** Spatial Ground.

**Always qualify when ambiguity is possible.**

Do not confuse with **Survey-reference validity**, which belongs to FSF.

---

## equivalence — Ground representations

**Meaning:** Two representations denote the same World-space.

**Owner:** Spatial Ground.

**Do not confuse with:** FSF reference equality.

---

## semantic identity

**Meaning:** Sameness of meaning.

**Owner:** Spatial Ground.

Semantic identity may persist when representation or artifact identity changes.

---

## artifact identity

**Meaning:** Sameness of a record as an object.

**Owner:** Spatial Ground / Atlas recordkeeping.

**Do not treat as:** proof of semantic identity.

Byte identity is not required for semantic identity.

---

# 4. Limit, Boundary, and Edge

## canonical limit / limit

**Meaning:** Where World membership changes, as a consequence of the canonical definition.

**Owner:** Spatial Ground.

**Preferred term:** canonical limit.

**Do not use for Ground's limit:** Boundary, edge, coast, coastline, border.

---

## Boundary

**Meaning:** A constitutional concept of The Codex.

**Owner:** Codex.

**Do not use as:** the Spatial Ground canonical limit.

---

## edge

**Meaning:** A perceived, rendered, geographic, visual, experiential, or otherwise higher-layer edge.

**Owner:** Higher layers.

**Do not use as:** a canonical Ground concept.

A higher-layer edge may coincide with the canonical limit without becoming identical to it.

# 5. Continuity, Connectedness, Permanence, and Refinement

## continuity

**Meaning:** The constitutional property that The World is one continuous whole.

**Owner:** Codex / constitutional sources.

**Do not use for:** persistence through time.

---

## connectedness

**Meaning:** Spatial Ground's formal expression of constitutional continuity using the topology and predicate inherited from FSF.

**Owner:** Ground for the expressed requirement; FSF for the mathematical predicate.

**Do not infer from connectedness:** hole-freedom, simple connectedness, convexity, boundary count, recognizable form, or any further topology.

---

## permanence

**Meaning:** Persistence of World membership over time once the canonical instance is adopted.

**Owner:** Spatial Ground.

**Use instead of:** continuity when discussing temporal persistence.

---

## precision

**Meaning:** The Survey Fabric's resolution of spatial reference.

**Owner:** FSF.

**Do not create:** a separate Ground precision system.

---

## refinement

**Meaning:** Deepening of Survey precision.

**Owner:** FSF for mechanics; Spatial Ground for refinement-closure.

For Spatial Ground, refinement enables finer **evaluation** of already-established truth.

**Do not say:** refinement reveals, discovers, creates, or newly decides membership.

# 6. Design, Discovery, Recognition, and Adoption

## design

**Meaning:** The Creator's choice, within constitutional constraint, of the exact permissible spatial expression.

**Owner:** Atlas.

**Use for:** the exact canonical membership-set choice before adoption.

**Do not describe that exact choice as:** discovered or constitutionally predetermined where the constitutional sources leave design room.

---

## discovery

**Meaning:** Finding constitutional truths and architectural requirements.

**Owner:** Atlas / constitutional inquiry according to context.

**Do not use for:** the exact designed World-space membership set.

---

## recognition

**Meaning:** Constitutional or Codex recognition of truth that already exists upstream.

**Owner:** Codex / constitutional sources.

**Do not use for:** Spatial Ground's layer function.

Spatial Ground **expresses and preserves**.

---

## candidate

**Meaning:** A proposed Spatial Ground definition that is explicitly noncanonical.

**Owner:** Atlas.

**Do not use:** draft canon, provisional canon.

A candidate may be revised or discarded without changing canonical truth.

---

## adoption / adopted

**Meaning:** The recorded institutional act that makes an artifact or definition canonical, and the resulting record state.

**Owner:** Atlas.

**Do not infer adoption from:** correctness, publication, implementation, use, certification, custody, popularity, technical control, or convention.

---

## established

**Meaning:** Synonym for adopted.

**Owner:** Atlas.

**Do not treat as:** an additional lifecycle state.

**Do not use for:** the layer's function.

---

## superseded

**Meaning:** A formerly primary artifact replaced by a designated successor.

**Owner:** Atlas.

For the canonical World-space instance, supersession may only preserve meaning.

**Do not imply:** that World-space itself was spatially replaced.

---

## record state

**Meaning:** Candidate, Adopted, or Superseded.

**Owner:** Atlas.

These are the canonical record states.

**Do not use:** state of Ground, state of World-space.

Review, dispute, correction proposal, maturity, archival condition, and operational condition are annotations, evidence conditions, or implementation conditions — not additional canonical record states.

---

## maturity

**Meaning:** A body of evidence concerning a Specification and its implementations.

**Owner:** Atlas / review framework.

**Do not use as:** a state of Ground or World-space.

---

## operational

**Meaning:** A condition of an implementation.

**Owner:** Implementers.

**Do not use as:** a canonical state of Spatial Ground.

Operational activation creates no canonical truth.

# 7. Change and Constitutional Return

## correction

**Meaning:** Restoring agreement with what the Adoption Act actually recorded.

**Owner:** Atlas governance.

**Do not use for:** changing a valid, unambiguous adopted definition.

Correction is not redesign.

---

## scope change

**Meaning:** Enlargement or reduction of Spatial Ground's architectural responsibilities.

**Owner:** Atlas governance / shared architecture.

**Preferred term:** scope change.

**Do not use the bare word:** expansion.

---

## materialization

**Meaning:** Production of derived representations.

**Owner:** Spatial Ground / implementations according to context.

**Do not call:** expansion.

Materialization does not enlarge World-space.

---

## extension of World-space

**Meaning:** Addition of Survey space to established World-space.

**Owner:** none within Spatial Ground, The Atlas, stewardship, implementation, ordinary governance, or post-Creator architectural succession.

No authority for this act exists within the Spatial Ground architecture.

**Do not use ordinary “expansion” language** for it.

---

## model replacement

**Meaning:** Adoption of a different formal model with proven continuity of meaning.

**Owner:** Atlas.

**Do not use:** revision when the intent is meaning-preserving formal-model replacement.

A model replacement may not alter established World-space membership.

---

## constitutional return

**Meaning:** Referring a question upward to constitutional inquiry because it depends on a constitutional matter.

**Owner:** Atlas routes; constitutional institutions decide.

**Do not treat as:** a Ground or ordinary Atlas decision on the constitutional substance itself.

---

## formal constitutional revision

**Meaning:** An upstream constitutional act explicitly contemplated by The Declaration's repository guidance, which preserves the foundational declaration unless a formal constitutional revision is made.

**Owner:** constitutional sources / whatever upstream authority may validly govern such an act.

**Outside this architecture:** its authority, procedure, scope, and substantive reach are not defined here.

**Do not:**

- describe it as a Spatial Ground change mechanism;
- describe it as an ordinary Atlas mechanism;
- invent its authority or procedure;
- assert that it is impossible.

---

## re-founding

**Meaning:** Not an act available within this architecture.

The term is retained only to state what reconstruction is **not**.

**Do not use as:** a recovery, correction, migration, or governance option.

# 8. Custody, Provenance, Integrity, and Evidence

## steward

**Meaning:** A recognized custodian with preservation duties and no semantic authority.

**Owner:** Atlas.

**Do not use as synonyms:** owner, authority, maintainer when those words would imply semantic power.

---

## custodian in fact

**Meaning:** An unrecognized holder of a faithful, verifiable copy.

**Owner:** Atlas terminology.

Such a copy may carry evidentiary value.

The holder acquires no canonical standing merely by possession.

---

## provenance

**Meaning:** The chain of recorded institutional acts linking a record to the Adoption Act.

**Owner:** Atlas.

**Do not reduce to:** history of possession, hosting, signatures, or custody.

---

## integrity

**Meaning:** Evidence that a copy remains unchanged from the record it claims to reproduce.

**Owner:** Atlas / Preservation.

**Do not treat as:** proof of authority, adoption, semantic identity, or meaning.

# 9. Recorded Terminology Hazards

The following terminology collisions are formally resolved for Spatial Ground drafting.

### Expansion

The bare word **expansion** is prohibited where ambiguity can arise.

Use instead:

- **scope change** — change in Ground responsibilities;
- **materialization** — production of derived representations;
- **extension of World-space** — addition of Survey space to established World-space, for which no architectural authority exists.

### Continuity

Use:

- **continuity** — constitutional spatial wholeness;
- **permanence** — temporal persistence of membership.

### State

Use:

- **record state** — Candidate, Adopted, Superseded.

Do not speak of a canonical “state of Ground” or “state of World-space.”

### Extent / limit / Boundary / edge

Keep four owners distinct:

- **Extent** — constitutional property of The World;
- **canonical limit** — Ground consequence of membership;
- **Boundary** — Codex concept;
- **edge** — higher-layer perceived or rendered edge.

### Establish / express

Use **express** for Spatial Ground's function.

Reserve **establish / established** for institutional adoption/status where appropriate.

### Discovery / design

Use:

- **discovery** — constitutional truths and architectural requirements;
- **design** — exact permissible Creator-period spatial choice.

### Canonical definition

Use **canonical definition** for the adopted World-space determination as meaning.

Do not use it for the Specification or rule set.

### Expression / representation

Use:

- **expresses** — the layer's function;
- **representation** — an encoding or artifact.

### Valid

Always qualify where needed:

- valid Survey reference — FSF;
- valid Spatial Ground definition — Ground.

### Equal / equivalent

Keep distinct:

- reference equality — FSF;
- semantic equivalence of Ground representations — Ground.

# 10. Drafting Rules

Spatial Ground drafting and every downstream framework record that refers to Spatial Ground must preserve these distinctions.

In particular:

1. use **express** rather than *establish* or *create* for Spatial Ground's relationship to World-space;
2. use **design** for the Creator's exact permissible spatial choice;
3. use **discovery** for constitutional truths and architectural necessities, not the exact designed membership set;
4. use **canonical limit** rather than Boundary or edge for the Ground consequence of membership;
5. use **permanence** for temporal persistence of membership;
6. preserve **continuity / connectedness** as constitutional property / Ground formal expression;
7. use **scope change** rather than ambiguous architectural expansion;
8. use **formal constitutional revision** only for an upstream constitutional act;
9. use **record state** rather than a state of World-space;
10. preserve **semantic identity** as distinct from artifact identity;
11. preserve **provenance** as distinct from integrity.

A term owned outside Spatial Ground may be referenced but must not be silently redefined here.
