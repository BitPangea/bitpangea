The Truths README lists 21 Truths. In particular 1–5, 7–10, 13–16 and 21 support the distinction between interpretation and creation. Origins Chapter V explicitly preserves exactly 21,000,000 canonical Parcels and distinguishes the World from its Cadastre.

## Disposition

No direct contradiction identified in the inspected definitions. Recommend strengthening AUTH-04 to cover all adopted concepts and explicitly distinguishing Survey expressions outside World-space from constitutional structures. Preserve Region and Cluster recognition without requiring their implementation in the initial profile. Do not equate geometric connectedness with Parcel Contiguity.
