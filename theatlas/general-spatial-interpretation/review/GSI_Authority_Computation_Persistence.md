# General Spatial Interpretation — Authority, Computation and Persistence

**Date:** November 26, 2026
**Status:** Working proposal — not adopted

## Principle

Preserve the authority. Reproduce the interpretation.

## Proposed model

GSI inherits authoritative FSF geometry and SG membership. GSI governs shared interpretation rules, subject to future adoption. Entity identity and authoritative association of an entity with geometry remain with the responsible domain.

A deterministic result is not necessarily a primary authoritative record. Computation is the default for reproducible relationship and participation results. Persistence is justified by a demonstrated evidence, continuity, audit or operational requirement, not merely by the existence of a result.

## Result context

A reproducible interpretation must identify its governing FSF and SG profiles, applicable Ground instance, GSI rule version when adopted, operation, and exact input meanings or resolvable immutable references. Where an external subject is involved, its authoritative geometry association must be identifiable. Historical reproduction requires preservation or recoverability of the applicable inputs and rules, not merely a hash or current mutable identifier.

## Derived artifacts

Indexes, caches, materialized results and visualizations are replaceable derived artifacts. They cannot override governing inputs or rules. A stale, invalid or unverifiable result must not silently substitute for a current valid interpretation. Disagreement requires investigation rather than creation of competing spatial truth.

Preserved evidence may document what was evaluated and under which context; preservation alone does not make a result an independent source of canonical geometry, membership or entity identity. No universal persistence schema, cryptographic mechanism or retention period is selected here. Coordinate with Persistence / Provenance when a demonstrated dependency emerges.

## Reservations

No canonical GSI serialization, mandatory registry, entity lifecycle, historical retention policy or new constitutional truth is adopted. Exact result contracts, error semantics and conformance remain open.
