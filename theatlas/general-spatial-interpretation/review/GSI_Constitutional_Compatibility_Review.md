# GSI — Constitutional Compatibility Review

**Date:** November 26, 2026
**Baseline:** Requirements R1
**Status:** Preliminary institutional compatibility review; detailed clause review pending

## Findings

The Foundation and Codex institutional records distinguish constitutional discovery from creative design. GSI belongs to Atlas architectural design within established constitutional constraints. It does not require a new Codex merely because its technical interpretation is useful.

The Structural Domain recognizes Parcel, Boundary, Coordinate and World. The Relational Domain recognizes Adjacency, Contiguity, Distance and Direction. Its relations do not assign ownership, jurisdiction, access or civilizational meaning. GSI geometric predicates must not replace these constitutional concepts.

The World Properties Domain recognizes The Extent, The Region and The Cluster. Excluding Region and Cluster as entity types from the initial GSI ontology is a technical scope decision, not a denial of their constitutional recognition. Their eventual architectural expression must respect the adopted Codices.

## Requirements disposition

R1 AUTH-01 through AUTH-06 and CORE-01/02 are consistent in direction with the institutional division of authority. No new constitutional inquiry is presently demonstrated necessary. Before adoption, explicitly verify the individual adopted Codices and foundational Truths, including the fixed Parcel constraint, and examine whether the Requirements need stronger protection for recognized World Properties. This review does not establish clause-level constitutional compliance.
