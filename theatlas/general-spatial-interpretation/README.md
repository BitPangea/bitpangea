## Architectural Role

General Spatial Interpretation provides a shared spatial language for describing:

- location;
- extent;
- geometry;
- relationship;
- World membership.

Its scope applies across non-Parcel structures while preserving the separation between foundational spatial truth and the things defined within it.

The source establishes that higher structures may reference canonical BitPangea space without thereby becoming part of the Survey Fabric.

## Purpose of This Space

General Spatial Interpretation exists to give higher World structures a common way to describe:

- where they are;
- what spatial extent they occupy;
- how they relate geometrically;
- whether they participate in BitPangea World-space.

It operates upon the enduring World-space established through **Spatial Ground** while remaining anchored to canonical Survey reference.

It must not create competing spatial truth.

---

## Governing Direction

The source defines the domain’s governing direction as deliberately bounded.

It may interpret and describe spatial entities by reference to the canonical Survey Fabric, but it must not:

- redefine the Survey Fabric;
- assume Parcel identity;
- impose ownership or rights;
- collapse distinct spatial classes into one ontology.

The source further states that the layer should provide shared spatial meaning without becoming the source of permanent spatial truth.
