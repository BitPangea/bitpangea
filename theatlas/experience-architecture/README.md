## Architectural Role

Experience Architecture is the architectural home for the systems through which occupants encounter the World.

The source identifies the domain as governing:

- replaceable interfaces;
- representations;
- navigation models;
- immersive systems;
- other experiential structures through which BitPangea may be visited and used.

Its purpose is to permit those experiential systems to evolve without binding permanent World truth to any one technology.

The page preserves the following working principle:

> **The Architecture records what the World requires to remain durable, extensible, and coherent without confusing implementation with constitutional truth.**

This principle is especially important for Experience Architecture because the domain contains systems expected to remain replaceable and evolvable.

## What Remains Open

Experience Architecture remains provisional.

The source explicitly leaves the following open to future architectural inquiry:

- internal models;
- interfaces;
- representational systems;
- navigation structures;
- experiential conventions.

The domain’s responsibility and boundary are recognized, but these internal forms are not yet fixed.

---

## Institutional Boundary

The page defines the meaning of provisional architectural standing:

> **A provisional architectural domain establishes a recognized responsibility and boundary without prematurely fixing the internal forms through which that responsibility will ultimately be realized.**

This boundary should remain intact.

A provisional domain is not undefined.

Its responsibility is recognized even while its internal realization remains open.

---

## Experience and Canonical World Truth

The page establishes a clear separation between experience and permanent World truth.

Experience systems may change.

Canonical World truth must not be altered merely because a particular interface, representation, navigation model, immersive system, or other experiential technology changes.

This is the central architectural constraint expressed by the source page.
