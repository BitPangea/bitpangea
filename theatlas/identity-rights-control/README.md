## Architectural Role

The purpose of this domain is to keep identity and authority structurally distinct from canonical place so that rights may evolve without altering the permanent spatial truth of BitPangea.

The source places the following within this domain:

- identity;
- rights;
- authorization;
- authority;
- control;
- delegation;
- restriction;
- transfer;
- revocation.

These relationships exist above canonical spatial and cadastral truth rather than becoming part of Parcel spatial identity, Parcel position, or the Foundational Survey Fabric.

## Purpose of This Space

Identity / Rights / Control exists to provide a durable architectural boundary between permanent place and the changing relationships that may govern:

- access;
- possession;
- stewardship;
- ownership;
- delegation;
- authority;
- control.

Parcels and other spatial entities may retain permanent identity and position even as the actors and rights associated with them change.

---

## Governing Direction

The source defines the domain’s governing direction as deliberately bounded.

The architecture may determine:

- who may act;
- what authority is recognized;
- how control is expressed.

But identity, rights, and control must not be embedded into the permanent spatial substrate itself.

Changes in these relationships should not:

- renumber canonical place;
- relocate canonical place;
- reinterpret canonical place;
- otherwise alter canonical spatial truth.
