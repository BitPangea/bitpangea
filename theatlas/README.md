## Overview

**The Atlas** is BitPangea’s canonical map and structural design record of the World.

It preserves what BitPangea currently is as a designed World: its enduring spatial architecture, cartographic form, Parcel structure, recognized Architecture domains, spatial relationships, and other structural design records that must remain intelligible independently of any one implementation or experience.

BitPangea is one finite World containing exactly **21,000,000 Parcels**.

The Atlas operates within constitutional truth established elsewhere. It does not create constitutional authority merely by recording or designing something.

The governing institutional progression is:

> **The Foundation constrains → The Codex defines → The Atlas designs → Implementation realizes.**

The standing editorial distinction is:

> **Chronicle records when. Atlas records what.**

The Atlas therefore records current structural understanding rather than narrating the sequence through which that understanding emerged.

---

## Purpose

The Atlas exists to design, preserve, and present the enduring structural expression of BitPangea within the constraints established by The Foundation and The Codex.

Its role includes:

- preserving the canonical structural design of the World;
- presenting BitPangea as one finite, coherent cartographic whole;
- recording adopted and provisional World-form design;
- preserving the Architecture through which permanent truth receives durable structural expression;
- developing and recording spatial systems beneath and above the Parcel Cadastre;
- preserving the canonical relationship among Survey reference, World-space, spatial interpretation, and Parcel truth;
- recording Parcel-related architecture for exactly **21,000,000 Parcels**;
- preserving recognized cartographic relationships, groupings, Regions, and other spatial structures;
- maintaining structural and cartographic records that remain intelligible across implementation change;
- distinguishing what is established, provisional, reserved, permissible, unresolved, or intentionally open;
- providing stable public paths for the World’s current structural record.

The Atlas may intentionally design features wherever constitutional truth leaves design freedom.

A design decision returns to constitutional inquiry only when deeper constitutional dependency genuinely requires it.

## Architecture Boundary Rule

The Atlas preserves the following rule:

> **A lower Architecture domain may establish the constraints that higher domains must not violate, but it should not prescribe the internal responsibilities, organization, implementation, or lifecycle of a higher domain unless that dependency is irreducibly necessary to the lower domain itself.**

Working form:

> **Protect the boundary. Do not design the layer above from the layer below.**

Examples:

- Survey should not become Cadastre.
- Cadastre should not become rights.
- rights should not become Runtime.
- Runtime should not become all authority.
- Experience should not become World truth.
