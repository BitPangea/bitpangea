## Purpose

This directory preserves **The Maxims**, the foundational collection of memorable expressions, sayings, reminders, and distilled wisdom of BitPangea.

The Maxims do not define the world and they do not govern conduct.

Their role is narrower:

> **Truths describe what is. Principles guide what should be. Maxims preserve what should be remembered.**

The public `index.html` serves as the active institutional page for this collection.

## Institutional Role

The Maxims is one of the eight foundational volumes preserved within **The Foundation**.

Its role is to preserve BitPangea’s enduring wisdom in a form that can be remembered, repeated, and carried forward.

The current distinction is:

```text
The Truths
→ constitutional realities

The Principles
→ stewardship doctrine

The Maxims
→ enduring sayings and distilled wisdom
```

The Maxims should remain memorable without becoming vague, doctrinal, or merely decorative.

## Boundary with Other Institutions

The Maxims should not:

- replace The Truths;
- replace The Principles;
- define constitutional terms;
- adopt Codices;
- preserve chronology;
- function as technical rules;
- become a collection of generic inspirational quotations;
- expand into commentary that overwhelms the memorable expression itself.

Its proper role is:

> **The Maxims preserves what BitPangea should remember.**

---

## Addition Standard

Before adding a new Maxim, confirm that it is:

- concise;
- memorable;
- distinct from an existing Maxim;
- broad enough to endure;
- specific enough to belong to BitPangea;
- not merely a Truth rewritten poetically;
- not merely a Principle shortened into a slogan;
- appropriate to one of the established thematic sections, or constitutionally strong enough to justify a new section.

When a new Maxim is adopted:

1. assign the next number;
2. place it in the correct thematic section;
3. add a brief interpretive sentence;
4. update this README;
5. record the adoption date in the relevant journal or Chronicle entry when appropriate.
