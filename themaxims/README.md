# The Maxims

**BitPangea**  
*A Digital Supercontinent*

---

## Overview

The Maxims preserves the enduring sayings, reminders, and distilled wisdom of BitPangea.

It is one of the foundational works preserved within **The Foundation**.

The Maxims do not define the World and they do not govern conduct.

Their role is narrower:

> Truths describe what is. Principles guide what should be. Maxims preserve what should be remembered.

---

## Purpose

The Maxims exists to preserve expressions that can be remembered, repeated, and carried forward.

A Maxim should be:

- concise;
- memorable;
- durable;
- distinct from a Truth;
- distinct from a Principle;
- specific enough to belong to BitPangea.

The Maxims should remain strong enough to endure without requiring extensive explanation.

---

## Institutional Role

The Maxims serves a distinct role within The Foundation:

```text
The Truths
→ constitutional realities

The Principles
→ stewardship doctrine

The Maxims
→ enduring sayings and distilled wisdom
```

The Maxims should not become a general collection of inspirational quotations.

Its purpose is to preserve the memorable essence of BitPangea.

## Repository Guidance

When maintaining this directory:

1. preserve the exact wording of adopted Maxims unless a formal revision is made;
2. keep numbering continuous;
3. keep each Maxim within its proper thematic section;
4. preserve the distinction among Truths, Principles, and Maxims;
5. verify all public links, image paths, and metadata;
6. preserve earlier significant versions before major revisions;
7. keep public documentation free of private maintenance notes or concealed discovery details;
8. document major additions or revisions in the appropriate BitPangea journal or Chronicle entry.

---

## Addition Standard

Before adding a new Maxim, confirm that it is:

- concise enough to remember;
- distinct from existing Maxims;
- broad enough to endure;
- specific enough to belong to BitPangea;
- not merely a Truth rewritten poetically;
- not merely a Principle shortened into a slogan;
- appropriate to an existing thematic section or strong enough to justify a new one.

---

## What The Maxims Is Not

The Maxims is not:

- a substitute for The Truths;
- a substitute for The Principles;
- a technical rulebook;
- a chronology;
- a general quotation collection;
- a repository for temporary slogans.

Its proper role is:

> **The Maxims preserves what BitPangea should remember.**
