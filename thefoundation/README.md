# The Foundation

**BitPangea**

## Overview

The Foundation is the constitutional library of BitPangea.

It preserves the principal works through which the World’s existence, purpose, structure, language, truths, principles, wisdom, and memory become intelligible.

The Foundation is not a single document.

It is the institutional shelf upon which BitPangea’s foundational works are preserved together.

---

## Purpose

The Foundation exists to maintain the relationship among BitPangea’s principal constitutional and philosophical works.

Its purpose is to provide a stable public index to the works that explain:

- that the World exists;
- why the World exists;
- how the World is understood;
- how its language is preserved;
- what remains true;
- how stewardship should be approached;
- what wisdom should be remembered;
- what historical materials should endure.

> The Foundation is the constitutional library of the World.

## Foundational Works

The Foundation currently preserves eight principal volumes.

### Volume I · Existence

**The Declaration**

The Declaration proclaims the existence and singularity of the World.

### Volume II · Purpose

**The Charter**

The Charter preserves the founding purpose and constitutional direction of BitPangea.

### Volume III · Structure

**The Codex**

The Codex preserves structured inquiry, recognized knowledge, and the conceptual architecture through which the World becomes precise.

### Volume IV · Language

**The Lexicon**

The Lexicon preserves the language, definitions, and institutional vocabulary of BitPangea.

### Volume V · Reality

**The Truths**

The Truths preserve the constitutional realities of the World.

### Volume VI · Stewardship

**The Principles**

The Principles preserve the stewardship doctrine through which the World should be approached and carried forward.

### Volume VII · Wisdom

**The Maxims**

The Maxims preserve the enduring sayings and distilled wisdom of BitPangea.

### Volume VIII · Memory

**The Archive**

The Archive preserves records, earlier versions, references, and historical materials of enduring value.

---

## Institutional Role

The Foundation does not replace the works it contains.

Each foundational volume retains its own purpose, authority, and public home.

The Foundation serves as:

- the constitutional library;
- the public index to the foundational works;
- the place where their relationship is made clear;
- the stable entry point for understanding BitPangea’s constitutional architecture.

The Foundation should remain cumulative rather than duplicative.

## Repository Guidance

When maintaining this directory:

1. preserve the order and titles of the foundational volumes;
2. keep public paths stable;
3. verify all links to the eight foundational institutions;
4. preserve earlier significant versions before major revisions;
5. verify image paths, metadata, canonical links, and shared framework assets;
6. keep public documentation free of private maintenance notes or concealed discovery details;
7. document major constitutional changes in the appropriate BitPangea journal or Chronicle entry.

---

## What The Foundation Is Not

The Foundation is not:

- a substitute for The Declaration;
- a substitute for The Charter;
- a substitute for The Codex;
- a duplicate repository for the contents of every foundational volume;
- a general navigation page for all BitPangea institutions;
- a technical specification;
- a roadmap.

Its role is to preserve the constitutional library as a coherent whole.
