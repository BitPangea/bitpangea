## Institutional Role

The Foundation preserves the constitutional and philosophical works upon which BitPangea is established.

Its current public architecture presents eight foundational volumes:

1. **The Declaration** — existence
2. **The Charter** — purpose
3. **The Codex** — structure
4. **The Lexicon** — language
5. **The Truths** — reality
6. **The Principles** — stewardship
7. **The Maxims** — wisdom
8. **The Archive** — memory

These works do not replace one another.

Together, they form the constitutional library through which BitPangea becomes intelligible.

## Boundary with Other Institutions

The Foundation should not:

- replace The Declaration;
- absorb the constitutional history preserved by The Charter;
- adopt or define Codices;
- duplicate the vocabulary of The Lexicon;
- restate every Truth, Principle, or Maxim;
- become a general archive;
- preserve chronology;
- function as a secondary navigation system for unrelated institutions.

Its role is precise:

> **The Foundation is the constitutional library of BitPangea.**

It preserves the relationship among the foundational works and provides the active public index through which they may be entered.
