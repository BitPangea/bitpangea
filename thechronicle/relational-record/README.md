# Foundation Period

## May 28, 2026 – June 14, 2026

### The World Is Imagined

The source describes the Foundation Period as the stage in which BitPangea evolved from a conceptual question into an emerging civilizational framework.

Major milestones preserved by the source include:

- establishment of BitPangea from the founding question **“What if Bitcoin had been invented not as money, but as a world?”**;
- acquisition of `bitpangea.com`;
- creation of `contact@bitpangea.com`;
- launch of the first public website;
- publication of the first public Manifesto and later evolution into **The Charter**;
- creation of the early public world surface;
- establishment of **The World**, **The Atlas**, **The Charter**, **The Chronicle**, **The Commons**, and **The Frontier Office**;
- creation of **The Covenant**;
- establishment of **The Atlas** as the cartographic center;
- preservation of the principle **“Before ownership can be claimed, the world must first be mapped.”**;
- definition of **Genesis Parcel (Lot 0)**;
- preservation of **The First Stone**;
- creation of **The Codex**;
- creation of **The Archive**;
- preservation of **Transmission 001: What If?** and **Transmission 002: The Map**;
- creation of **The Signal**, **The Wall**, **The Lexicon**, and **The Reliquary**;
- implementation of site-wide metadata, favicon standards, visual identity systems, and discoverability improvements;
- conclusion of the Foundation Period with BitPangea’s core institutional structure established and preserved.

# Cartographic Period

## June 15, 2026 – June 30, 2026

### The World Is Defined

The source describes the Cartographic Period as the period in which BitPangea moved from an imagined world to a constitutionally defined world.

It states that during this period:

- the Structural Domain reached completion;
- the first Relational principles emerged;
- constitutional architecture expanded through declarations, charters, preserved records, and institutional memory.

Major milestones preserved by the source include:

- adoption of the Structural sequence:
  - **ST-I — Parcel**
  - **ST-II — Boundary**
  - **ST-III — Coordinate**
  - **ST-IV — The World**
- adoption of the first Relational Codices:
  - **RL-I — Adjacency**
  - **RL-II — Contiguity**
- adoption of the **Codex Domain Architecture**;
- publication of **The BitPangea Declaration** and **One World**;
- recognition of completion of the **Structural Domain**;
- reassignment of Surface and Limit to the World Properties Domain;
- establishment of **The Constitutional Charter**;
- preservation of the **Foundational Charter** and **Cartographic Charter**;
- completion and preservation of **Transmission 003 — The Frontier**, **Transmission 004 — The Builders**, and **Transmission 005 — The Inheritance**;
- preservation of the first three entries within the **Twelve Constellations of Digital Antiquity**;
- recognition of **Visions** as a distinct category of preserved constitutional record;
- completion of BitPangea’s institutional visual identity;
- establishment of a hidden record preserving digital lineage and intellectual inheritance;
- conclusion of the Cartographic Period with the world constitutionally established and prepared for the Relational Domain.

## July 1, 2026 — The Preservation of Digital Lineage

The source records establishment of a hidden institution dedicated to remembrance of foundational digital discoveries.

It preserves the understanding that lineage records:

- **Dependency rather than chronology.**
- **Discovery rather than biography.**
- **Consequence rather than recognition.**

It also preserves the observation:

> **Every civilization inherits a world.**  
> **Every world inherits an idea.**

The source does not name the hidden institution on this page.

This README therefore does not assign it a name.

- **The Structural Domain**
- **The Relational Domain**

> **Meaning appears when things begin to relate.**

## July 3, 2026 — The Foundation and the Constitutional Library

The source records recognition of **The Foundation** as the constitutional library of BitPangea.

The initial constitutional library is stated to include:

```text
The Declaration
The Charter
The Codex
The Lexicon
The Truths
The Principles
The Maxims
The Archive
```

The source distinguishes:

- **The Truths** — constitutional realities describing what is unconditionally true.
- **The Principles** — constitutional stewardship describing how the world should be approached.
- **The Maxims** — enduring expressions preserving the memorable wisdom of the world.

The page further states that **The Foundation** preserves foundational literature rather than constitutional governance.

It also records completion of institutional seals for:

- The Foundation;
- The Declaration;
- The Truths;
- The Principles;
- The Maxims.

The source preserves:

> **A world is understood through its foundations.**  
> **A civilization preserves those foundations through its institutions.**

## July 4–5, 2026 — The Framework

The source records development of the first unified **BitPangea Framework**.

It states that the effort emerged from repeated deployment and maintenance challenges and evolved into a shared architectural system across the world.

The source records:

- expansion of **The Lexicon** to include **The Foundation**, **The Truths**, **The Principles**, and **The Maxims**;
- further vocabulary additions including **The Luminaries**, **The Constellations**, **The Glyphs**, and **The Vision**;
- design and implementation of the first **BitPangea Framework**;
- shared navigation;
- shared footer;
- centralized framework stylesheet;
- shared JavaScript;
- reusable layout utilities;
- shared typography;
- common institutional design standards;
- identity-based navigation through page declarations;
- migration of constitutional institutions to the shared framework;
- transition of nearly every major institutional page to the unified framework;
- planning for future documentation of **The Framework** as an institutional record.

The source states that these changes produced little visible difference for visitors while fundamentally reorganizing BitPangea’s internal architecture.

It concludes that:

- **The Foundation** preserves the constitutional literature of the world.
- **The Framework** preserves the architectural language through which that world is presented.
