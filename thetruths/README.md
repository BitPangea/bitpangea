# The Truths

**BitPangea**  
*A Digital Supercontinent*

---

## Overview

The Truths preserves the constitutional realities of BitPangea.

It is one of the foundational works maintained within **The Foundation**.

The Truths does not instruct conduct, establish aspirations, or preserve cultural sayings. It states what is understood to be unconditionally true of the World.

> Truths describe what is. Principles guide what should be. Maxims preserve what should be remembered.

---

## Purpose

The Truths exists to preserve the realities upon which later constitutional and institutional works depend.

These realities remain foundational regardless of:

- discovery;
- ownership;
- recognition;
- belief;
- interpretation;
- the passage of time.

Where The Codex recognizes and defines concepts, The Truths preserves the conditions that make those concepts possible.

---

## Institutional Role

The Truths serves a distinct role within The Foundation:

```text
The Truths
→ constitutional realities

The Principles
→ stewardship doctrine

The Maxims
→ enduring sayings and distilled wisdom
```

The Truths is descriptive rather than prescriptive.

It establishes the constitutional floor upon which later Principles, Maxims, Codices, and institutions may stand.

## Repository Guidance

When maintaining this directory:

1. preserve the exact wording of adopted Truths unless a formal constitutional revision is made;
2. keep numbering continuous;
3. keep each Truth within its proper thematic section;
4. preserve the distinction among Truths, Principles, Maxims, and Codex definitions;
5. verify all public links, image paths, metadata, and shared framework assets;
6. preserve earlier significant versions before major revisions;
7. keep public documentation free of private maintenance notes or concealed discovery details;
8. document major additions or revisions in the appropriate BitPangea journal or Chronicle entry.

---

## Addition Standard

Before adding a new Truth, confirm that it is:

- descriptive rather than prescriptive;
- constitutionally foundational;
- distinct from an existing Truth;
- independent of individual belief or preference;
- broader than a domain-specific Codex definition;
- durable enough to remain true across later Periods;
- appropriate to an existing thematic section or strong enough to justify a new one.

---

## What The Truths Is Not

The Truths is not:

- an ethical code;
- a stewardship doctrine;
- a collection of Maxims;
- a technical specification;
- a chronology;
- a substitute for The Codex;
- a place for unresolved questions or aspirations.

Its proper role is:

> **The Truths preserves the constitutional realities of BitPangea.**
