## Overview

The Covenant is BitPangea’s public statement of restraint, clarification, and present condition.

It exists to distinguish the World as currently conceived from any future implementation, offering, allocation, or technical system.

The Covenant preserves a simple but important boundary:

> There is a difference between imagining a World and implementing one.

## Purpose

The Covenant exists to help visitors understand what BitPangea is today and what it is not.

Its role is to preserve:

- clarity about the project’s current stage;
- restraint against premature speculation;
- separation between constitutional imagination and technical implementation;
- accurate public expectations;
- continuity as BitPangea develops over time.

The Covenant should remain direct, current, and publicly understandable.
