# The Codex Register

**Established:** June 19, 2026  
**Period:** Cartographic Period · Creator Period Clarified

---

## Purpose

The Codex Register records formal entries adopted into The Codex.

It exists as the permanent historical record of constitutional definitions recognized within BitPangea.

The Register does not create Codices.

The Register preserves them.

Beginning with the Creator Period, the Register is understood more precisely as a record of concepts that have earned **independent constitutional standing**. It is not a catalogue of every feature that may legitimately exist within the World.

---

## Register Principle

The Codex Register does not define the World by itself.

It records the constitutional definitions adopted into The Codex.

> **“Not registered as a Codex” means “not constitutionally required.” It does not mean “not allowed to exist.”**

## Scope

The Codex Register preserves:

- canonical Domain and Codex identifier;
- Codex title;
- adoption date;
- historical sequence;
- later revision or refinement dates where relevant.

The Register does not preserve as adopted Codices:

- draft concepts;
- proposed definitions;
- working discussions;
- research candidates;
- no-adoption outcomes;
- Atlas design features;
- implementation choices;
- unadopted entries.

Only formally adopted Codices are recorded.

A concept may still be historically important, useful in research, or intentionally realized through design without appearing in the Register.

---

## Historical Role

As BitPangea evolves, The Codex Register serves as the canonical historical record of adopted constitutional definitions.

Future generations may consult the Register to understand:

- when a definition was adopted;
- the Domain to which it belongs;
- the sequence of constitutional development;
- the evolution of BitPangea's foundational concepts;
- which concepts were elevated into constitutional law rather than remaining design, research, or implementation choices.

The Register therefore records constitutional selection as much as constitutional accumulation.

---

## Creator Period Clarification

The Creator Period establishes a deliberate boundary between constitutional adoption and intentional design.

The Register remains on the **constitutional track**:

**Foundation → Necessary Consequences → Constitutional Inquiry → Codex / No-Adoption**

Creative world-building proceeds on a separate but compatible track:

**Constitutional Constraints → Creative Design → Atlas Expression → Implementation → Observation of Consequences → Constitutional Trigger if necessary**

The Atlas may therefore create geography, terrain, naming systems, visual forms, Parcel arrangements, landmarks, and other design features without requiring those features to appear in The Codex Register.

A design choice should return to constitutional inquiry only when changing it would alter identity, permanence, an adopted Codex, or another foundational dependency.

## Current Domain Standing

- **Structural Domain:** presently complete at the first-order foundational level; constitutionally open.
- **Relational Domain:** presently complete in its first-order relational grammar; constitutionally open.
- **World Properties Domain:** open and presently resting; no immediate inquiry constitutionally compelled.

An open Domain does not require continuous Codex production.

---

## Permanence

Once a Codex is formally adopted and entered into The Codex Register, its historical entry remains part of the permanent record.

New Codices may be added only when genuine constitutional necessity is demonstrated.

Historical adoption records are preserved even when later architectural review clarifies the standing of their Domains.

## Doctrine

> Definitions establish meaning.  
> The Register preserves memory.

> The absence of a Codex is not the absence of permission to create.
