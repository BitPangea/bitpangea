## Historical Integrity

Origins is a historical foundational publication.

Its purpose is not to be continuously rewritten until it always matches the latest Atlas wording.

When current BitPangea architecture advances beyond statements preserved in Origins:

- preserve the historical publication;
- update The Atlas to reflect current architecture;
- use later Origins versions, appendices, errata, or explanatory notes when necessary;
- do not silently rewrite Version 0.1 merely to erase the historical development path.

The governing documentary principle remains:

**Origins preserves how and why. Atlas records what. Chronicle records when.**

When maintaining `/origins/`:

1. preserve Version 0.1 as a stable historical publication;

8. correct genuine errors transparently when necessary;
