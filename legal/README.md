# BitPangea Legal

The `/legal/` directory contains BitPangea’s public legal notice and related legal-status documentation.

Its purpose is to provide a clear public record of what BitPangea presently is, what it is not, and how its published materials should be interpreted.

---

## Public Legal Page

The primary public legal notice is published at:

**https://bitpangea.com/legal/**

Repository path:

```text
/legal/index.html
```

The public page provides general notice concerning:

- BitPangea’s current project status;
- the meaning and present status of the exactly **21,000,000 Parcels**;
- the absence of any present Parcel sale, Parcel reservation system, token sale, securities offering, or investment product;
- the absence of any finalized blockchain, ownership, governance, rights, control, marketplace, or World Runtime implementation;
- the distinction between conceptual, constitutional, architectural, historical, provisional, experimental, and implemented materials;
- intellectual-property rights;
- third-party rights and external references;
- reliance, warranty, and limitation-of-liability notices;
- future service-specific terms;
- and contact information for legal or rights-related inquiries.

## Relationship to `LEGAL.md`

BitPangea also maintains a repository-level legal record at:

```text
/LEGAL.md
```

The two records serve complementary purposes.

### `/LEGAL.md`

The repository-level legal notice.

It provides the full legal and public-status record in Markdown form and may be referenced directly from repository documentation.

### `/legal/index.html`

The public-facing website presentation of the legal notice.

It presents the same general legal posture in BitPangea’s public visual language.

Neither record should be interpreted as creating a sale, investment offering, ownership interest, contractual right, or promise of future implementation.

## Relationship to The Covenant

BitPangea also maintains **The Covenant**.

The Covenant and the Legal Notice are intentionally distinct.

**The Covenant** preserves BitPangea’s institutional statement of restraint, clarification, and present condition.

**The Legal Notice** provides general legal and public-status clarification.

The Covenant is institutional.

They may reinforce one another, but they should not be merged into one record.

## Governing Public Status

BitPangea is an experimental digital-world, cartographic, constitutional, architectural, research, and creative project under active development.

Nothing published by BitPangea should be interpreted as:

- an offer to sell Parcels;
- a Parcel reservation;
- a token sale or token distribution;
- a securities or investment offering;
- investment, legal, tax, or financial advice;
- a promise of appreciation, liquidity, economic return, or future market value;
- a finalized blockchain implementation;
- a completed ownership, governance, rights, or control system;
- or a guarantee that any contemplated future system will be implemented.

References to future systems, ownership, markets, rights, governance, civilization, infrastructure, Bitcoin, cryptography, blockchain, or related technologies must be interpreted according to their stated project status.

## The 21,000,000 Parcels

BitPangea is founded upon the permanent project constraint of exactly:

**21,000,000 Parcels**

That number is part of BitPangea’s constitutional and architectural identity.

However, the existence of the Parcel concept does not by itself create:

- legal title;
- ownership rights;
- possession;
- a reservation;
- transferable property;
- a security;
- a financial instrument;
- or a promise of future economic value.

Any future system that creates legal, contractual, economic, technical, or operational rights concerning Parcels would require separate implementation and, where appropriate, separate governing terms.

## Repository Guidance

When maintaining `/legal/`:

1. preserve the public legal page at `/legal/index.html`;
2. keep `/LEGAL.md` aligned with material changes to the public legal posture;
3. preserve historically significant legal revisions where appropriate;
4. avoid silently changing material legal statements without a documented reason;
5. distinguish general project notice from future service-specific terms;
6. keep The Covenant separate from the Legal Notice;
7. keep legal language factual and restrained;
8. do not imply the existence of products, rights, markets, services, or obligations that BitPangea has not actually implemented.
