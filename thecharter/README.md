# The Charter

**BitPangea**  
*A Digital Supercontinent*

---

## Overview

The Charter preserves the founding purpose, constitutional direction, and major adopted statements of BitPangea.

It is the institutional record through which the World’s purpose is expressed and carried forward as BitPangea develops through its major Periods and Domains.

The Charter began with a simple founding question:

> What if Bitcoin had been invented not as money, but as a world?

---

## Purpose

The Charter exists to preserve why BitPangea was founded and what principles guide its development.

It establishes BitPangea as:

- one unified digital World;
- finite rather than endlessly expandable;
- built around permanence rather than disposability;
- guided by stewardship rather than mere possession;
- developed slowly enough for its institutions and structures to earn their place.

The Charter should remain broad enough to guide the World while avoiding technical claims that have not yet been constitutionally established.

---

## Foundational Direction

BitPangea is not primarily conceived as a game, metaverse, or speculative land project.

It is an exploration of what may emerge when digital geography is treated as finite, persistent, and meaningful.

The founding vision emphasizes:

### Scarcity

The World is finite.

### Permanence

The World and its records should be designed to endure.

### Neutrality

No temporary administrator should possess arbitrary authority over the World’s constitutional structure.

### Stewardship

Those who inherit the World also inherit responsibility for preserving it.

---

## The Shared World

BitPangea is conceived as one digital supercontinent rather than a collection of disconnected worlds.

That unity matters.

It creates a shared spatial reality in which relation, proximity, distinction, memory, and future civilization may become meaningful.

> What kind of civilization might emerge if digital history began with one bounded World?

---

## Constitutional Role

The Charter is distinct from other foundational institutions:

- **The Declaration** proclaims that the World exists.
- **The Charter** preserves why the World exists and the constitutional direction of its development.
- **The Codex** preserves canonical inquiry and recognized knowledge.
- **The Lexicon** preserves the language of the World.
- **The Archive** preserves materials of enduring historical value.
- **The Chronicle** preserves the chronology of BitPangea’s development.

The Charter should not duplicate those institutions.

Its role is to preserve purpose, constitutional direction, and formally adopted period records.

---

## Charter Development

The Charter may expand as BitPangea progresses through major constitutional Periods.

New Charter records should be added only when a Period, Domain, or major architectural transition has been sufficiently understood to justify formal preservation.

Each addition should:

1. identify the constitutional subject clearly;
2. preserve the reason for the record;
3. avoid speculative detail that has not been adopted;
4. remain consistent with previously established foundational works;
5. be documented in the appropriate journal or Chronicle entry.

---

## Repository Guidance

When maintaining this directory:

1. use clear and stable filenames;
2. preserve earlier significant versions when major revisions occur;
3. keep public-facing documentation free of private maintenance notes and concealed discovery details;
4. verify all public links and asset paths;
5. update canonical and social metadata when paths change;
6. distinguish active Charter records from preserved historical versions;
7. document major constitutional additions or revisions in the appropriate BitPangea records.

---

## What The Charter Is Not

The Charter is not:

- a technical specification;
- a roadmap;
- a token document;
- a land-allocation policy;
- a substitute for The Codex;
- a substitute for The Declaration;
- a repository for every unresolved idea.

Its purpose is constitutional rather than operational.
