## The Frontier

The Foundational Charter begins with:

> **We stand at the edge of a new frontier.**

It contrasts BitPangea with digital worlds characterized by:

- abundance without limit;
- infinite land;
- temporary ownership;
- centralized control.

It proposes instead:

- a single, unified digital supercontinent;
- territory scarce by design;
- a persistent world;
- a fixed number of parcels;
- cryptographic rules that cannot be changed on a whim.

The source states:

> **Just as Bitcoin introduced scarcity to digital money, BitPangea seeks to introduce scarcity to digital geography.**
