The Constitutional Record of BitPangea

The Charter serves as the constitutional record of BitPangea. It preserves the founding charter and the constitutional charters adopted through the World’s major constitutional periods, while also recording later methodological transitions that change how BitPangea develops.

Constitutional Purpose The Charter preserves what each constitutional era established — and distinguishes those constitutional achievements from later periods of intentional world design.

The Constitutional Charters

Each Charter preserves a different layer of BitPangea's constitutional development. The Foundational Charter establishes the original vision. The Cartographic, Relational, and World Properties Charters recognize what their completed constitutional periods discovered, clarified, and preserved.

These records do not replace one another. They form a cumulative constitutional history.

Beginning with the emergence of the Creator perspective on Day #99, BitPangea now distinguishes constitutional periods from periods whose principal work is creative design. A new Period does not require a new constitutional Charter merely because it exists.

The Creator Period

Beginning on Day #100, BitPangea enters a new mode of development. The Creator Period is not presently a new constitutional Domain and does not require an adopted Charter merely because a new Period has begun.

Its governing distinction is that constitutional discovery determines what the World must be, while creative design determines what may be intentionally created within those truths. The Foundation constrains. The Codex defines. The Atlas designs. Implementation realizes.
